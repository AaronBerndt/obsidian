# Tefnare The Reforged

Half-Elf Monk from the desert. Stole a powerful artifact (a *key*, see below) and is hunted by the Covenant of the Sword. From Dresk. **Currently alive.**

> Note on spelling: PlayerPi has written this as "Drisk" and "Drask" in different messages; campaign canon is **Dresk**. PlayerPi also uses "Quarum" and "Corum" interchangeably — treating these as the same deity unless clarified.

---

## Backstory (from PlayerPi)

### The original Sect — welcoming, then taken over
- Tef was born into a Sect in the Dresk region that was openly welcoming to outsiders. As a half-elf she felt accepted.
- A **radical covenant formed between sects** and took over Tef's home sect. Tef lived under them for ~a decade as a "freak / untouchable."
- The covenant became focused on **conquest and glory to Quarum/Corum** rather than the sect's original sacred duty.
- DM note from player: *"You can use them like a secret sect of enemies. Like zealot ninjas of the Covenant of the Sword. Or some other name."*

### The Tidus cleric and the spear (older draft)
- Tidus missionaries targeted the Dresk region because of Tidus's **"Rebirth" domain**.
- One specific Tidus cleric visited the Sect multiple times. On the final visit there was an argument and **the cleric was executed**.
- **The spear that killed the cleric is the spear Tef now carries.** This event triggered Tef leaving.
- Open question: was Tef directly responsible, indirectly, or just in the crowd? Was the cleric's death a deliberate catalyst?
- Player floats: Tef's fire abilities may be tied to Tidus somehow — *but* her flames are **not** silvery-blue.

### The Cult of the Sword takeover & the stolen key (current/preferred draft)
- After the **Cult of the Sword** took over her coven, Tef was **imprisoned in an enforced boot camp**.
- Doctrine: *"You're unfit to serve as Quarum's spear. You will either become of worth or die."*
- The regimen nearly killed her several times.
- Delirious from exhaustion, Tef had a revelation: her true trial was to **protect Quarum's legacy — the Spear of Kigeth**, stored within the **Vault of Vaneeroth**.
- The Vault can only be opened with **both Keys of the Ancients**.
- Tef stole **one key** and fled. She collapsed from exhaustion in the desert — which is where Ygara found her (consistent with Ygara's backstory in `characters.md`).
- Player flagged this as flexible: "key, keys, spear" — the vault could hold the **Rod piece and other artifacts**, *or* it could be the **gravesite where Yeenoghu was pinned down / killed**.

### The Drisk/Dresk port town encounter — Id'jakun
- At the port town of Dresk, Tef and Ygara meet **Id'jakun**, a wizard of the Dresk branch of Egoluis's academy.
- Id'jakun suggests Tef hand the key over to the academy's main branch (or to Egoluis directly) for safekeeping.
- **During the party's rest, Id'jakun secretly swaps the real key with a simulacrum.**
- Id'jakun is: **power-hungry, pompous, secretly evil, rival to Egoluis, rags-to-power arc.**
- Player flag: **Id'jakun may become PlayerPi's next character.**

## DM Plot Chain (Aaron's plan)

Tef is alive going in. The chain doesn't *require* her death, just leaves the door open.

1. **Something drives the party to commit to Dresk.** Original sketch had this be Tef's death — since she's still alive, the lever is now her *capture, near-miss, or personal escalation* (Covenant closes the net, Id'jakun's setup pays off, a Void Rift threatens her, etc.).
2. **Enter Liquid.** Liquid is either at Dresk or en route. He is hunting **knowledge to complete his magnum opus spell: "raise the city."**
3. **Liquid leaves the party — betrayal.**
4. **The party finds him raising the city.** Only at this point do they realize:
   - what Liquid was actually doing
   - that the **Rod piece is inside the city**
   - **Liquid himself does not know about the Rod**
5. **Epic chase into the rising city** — climax/setpiece. *This is a strong candidate location to fire Tef's break/death beat if you want to use it.*
