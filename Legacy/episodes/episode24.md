## Secrets and Clues
- `Egoluis`'s research places the next `Rod of Seven Parts` piece in `Dresk` — and he suspects it is the gigantic spear piercing the outer shell of `Yeenoghu`'s pyramid.
- `Red Hand` agents are in `Julee` hiring desert adventurers to breach the pyramid ruins ahead of the party.
- Desert traders whisper of an albino gnoll serving the death priests at the obsidian obelisk — a match for `Gamal`, the son `Ygara` believed dead.
- `Tef`'s mother marches with the `Cult of the Sword` hunting party, convinced she can still "correct" her daughter.
- `Tef`'s father, the brigand lord, has been selling word of her movements to the `Cult of the Sword` to save his own skin.
- The river of blood pouring from `Yeenoghu`'s pyramid has run faster and blacker since the `Void Rifts` appeared, and the `Dusk Elves` read it as their god stirring.
- Another independent covenant fell to the `Cult of the Sword` last month — only a handful now stand outside its rule.
- Among the harpies' plundered wreckage lies a `Dusk Elf` blade of `Cult of the Sword` make — proof the hunters have already crossed the sea.
- The death priests of the `Scar` proclaim that a "proper vessel" for `Yeenoghu`'s resurrection has finally been found.
- A rumor in `Julee` promises that drinking from the pyramid's blood river grants the strength of a god — but those who try are never seen again.
