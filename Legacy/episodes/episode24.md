## Secrets and Clues
- `Julee` is ruled by the `Merchant guild`  and it's motto is `Everything has a price`, in general they are cool with those who are ok with captialism and not messing with the flow of coin.
- The land of `Dresk`  has been warped by thousands of years of war and the fabric of reality has warped in certain areas, very little points of light exist in the desserts.
- `Red Hand` agents are in `Julee` hiring desert adventurers to breach the pyramid ruins ahead of the party.
- Desert traders whisper of an albino gnoll serving the death priests at the obsidian obelisk — a match for `Gamal`, the son `Ygara` believed dead.
- `Tef`'s mother marches with the `Cult of the Sword` hunting party, convinced she can still "correct" her daughter.
- `Tef`'s father, the brigand lord, has been selling word of her movements to the `Cult of the Sword` to save his own skin.
