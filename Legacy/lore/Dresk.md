The Region of Dresk was once an elven nation. They were ruled and empowered by warlocks pulling from the power of Yeenoghu, a black beastial God. Housed in a great pyramid at the base of the mountains that separate the region to the south.

Egyptian inspired infrastructure, forests, rivers, and lakes. Other cultures exist of course.

Quarum wanted to free Yeenoghu, blessed an army from Ostermark's predecessor.  The war destroyed the land. And the loss of Yeenoghu power desertified further.

Quarum impressed with their resilience blessed the few survivors. Who now call themselves Dusk Elves.

The Pyramid that held Yeenoghu is now one of many ruins, a gigantic spear piercing it's outer shell. Blood said to be Yeenoghu own, slowly pours like a river. 

About 500 ish years ago. Only a few elves even remember the old ways. The knowledge of rebuilding their nation has been severely crushed and replaced with religious fever. Various tribes now litter the region, each one a different denomination of their religion.
The cult of the sword, a more radical group started conquering smaller covenants. The few who remain are either strong enough to stand alone, or are too hard to find or get to.

Tefnari's own was assimilated almost 10 years ago. Her half blood was seen as impure, and was treated with harsher scrutiny under the Cult of the sword. 

Tef's father is a brigand lord. A selfish man willing to sacrifice others to save his own skin.

Tef's mother is a convert into the Cult. She is convinced that both the cult is just, and that Tef is either misunderstood, or able to be corrected.
Cult believe's in purity in Strength, to become a weapon for Quarum to use. Any action is an action to strengthen one self so that you may be of use to Quarum if he calls upon you. Which could be here and now.

Traditionalist beliefs are similar. A real weapon should be one that has achieved inner balance. With the understanding that perfection of form is the path, not the destination. And that competition is the spirit of Quarum, not the consequences of.
The only true civilization is Julee, where adventures gather to set fourth into the ruin scattered desert. Seeking fortune or mysteries.
