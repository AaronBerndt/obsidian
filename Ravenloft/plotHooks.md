# Plot Notes & Hooks

## Core Campaign Spine [VRGtR structure, Homebrew detail]
**Arrive → Get an invitation → Survive the Masquerade → Uncover Saidra's secret → Escape or Unmask**

1. **Arrival:** The PCs come out of the Mists on the docks. Everyone is beautifully dressed, and nobody will admit anything is wrong.
2. **Social climbing:** To get what they need (the MacGuffin, a lost companion, the way out), they have to earn an invitation to the Masquerade. Options: salons, a patron on the Council, the Red Widow, or a bargain with the hags.
3. **The first Masquerade:** A guest is unmasked and turned to dust in front of everyone, and the party learns the rules.
4. **Digging deeper:** Chateaufaux isn't real, the food supply is a mystery, the Red Death walks at night, and the Duke's room stays locked.
5. **Climax:** Expose Saidra (her real fear), break her illusion, or escape through a Mistway. Unmasking her in front of the whole ball might be the only way to hurt her.

## Opening Hooks [Homebrew]
- **The Invitation:** The PCs wake up in the Mists already holding an invitation to this week's Masquerade, made out to people they've never heard of.
- **The Dust Murders:** A Watch captain (Baron Morisse's man) hires outsiders to investigate people turning to dust. Every lead points toward the Duchess.
- **Missing Dancer:** A girl from the alleys disappeared after a private show at the Red Widow, and her mother begs the party for help.
- **The Detective:** Alanik Ray [Classic] is working the same case and wants the PCs as his legwork.

## Mid-Campaign Threads [Homebrew]
- **Hunger:** The city's hidden food supply is running out. Riots are coming, and masks are slipping.
- **The Hag Debt:** A PC (or an ally) takes a deal at Three Odd Gables, and the catch arrives at midnight.
- **The Brother's Ghost:** The Duke's spirit wants Saidra to admit what happened. He could be an ally, or a key to her destruction.
- **The Father at the Docks:** A drunk old man claims to be the "exiled duke" and Saidra's father. Saidra protects him, and he's leverage.
- **Council Coup:** One of the Five plots to replace Saidra and wants the PCs as disposable tools.
- **The Old Darklord:** A portrait of **Dominic d'Honaire** [Classic] in the estate has eyes that follow people. Is he trapped, or waiting to come back?
- **The Godmother:** A kind old woman offers help with no strings attached. She's the same spirit that "helped" Saidra, meaning the Dark Powers.

## Jazz Age Threads [Homebrew]
See [[JazzAge]].
- **The Ash Trade:** Someone is buying the ash-sweepers' sacks of dust by the cartload. Following the buyer leads somewhere awful.
- **The Season:** The PCs have to climb the social calendar, from the Opéra opening to the Regatta to the White Ball, to earn their way to the Masquerade. Each event is a social encounter with its own rivals and dangers.
- **The Ruined Host:** A noble spends everything on one last spectacular party. The PCs are invited, and the host plans for no one to leave.
- **The Watching Mask:** Someone is painting new eyes on the billboard over La Cendrée, and each new pair matches a person who gets dusted the following night.

## Themes to Hit
- **Impostor syndrome:** Make the PCs pretend too. Give rewards for staying in character at social events and consequences for slipping.
- **Masks:** Literal and figurative. Who is the PC under their mask?
- **The cost of appearances:** Show hungry children in silk. Show nobles selling their teeth.
- **Midnight:** Midnight is when the plague struck. Use it for every reveal.

## Session Zero Notes [Homebrew]
- Give each PC a **mask** of their own and a secret they're hiding.
- Settle on a tone: courtly intrigue vs. gothic horror. Dementlieu does both.
- Ask each player: "What does your character pretend to be?"
