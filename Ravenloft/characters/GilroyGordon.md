# Gilroy Gordon, "Ser Gordon the Gilded"

**Player**: *(not given yet)*
**Pronouns**: he/him
**Role**: Patrol captain in the city
**Creator**: Madame Scarlet, a persona of one of the godmothers ([[ThreeOddGables|Three Odd Gables]])

The perfect chivalrous knight, made by a hag as a sleeper agent against the Duchess. He
didn't know it until his creators came calling.

## Origins

- The hag **Madame Scarlet** made him to be the perfect chivalrous knight.
- He was meant to work his way into the court through loyalty and charm.
- He is the godmothers' plant, in case they ever need to use him against the Duchess or remove her.
- **Until now, he didn't know his origins or what he physically is.** He had only ever known his life as a dutiful knight.

## Madame Scarlet

Known for her iconic long red hair, which is rumored to be dyed with mortal blood.

She isn't a separate hag. She's one of the personas a hag of the coven shifts between,
so Gilroy's creator may also be a hag he knows by another name.

## The Godmothers

- He has yet to serve his true purpose.
- He has known about the godmothers, but he has never needed to seek them out himself.

## The Masquerade

He has never formally attended the [[DHonaireEstate|Masquerade]], but he has stood guard
there as part of his peacekeeping duties. He hopes he might receive an invitation one day
in honor of his service.

## The Conspiracy

- **Why he's in**: The hags are calling on him to serve his purpose.
- **How he feels about it**: He doesn't know what to think about his change of circumstances.
- **What he fears**: That if he doesn't do what his creators want, he will be **unmade**.
- **His only chance**: To serve as well as he can and hope there's a place for him when the dust settles.
