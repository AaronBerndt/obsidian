# Crimson Fall

**Player**: RielDeal
**Pronouns**: she/her
**Role**: Facilitator for the godmothers of [[ThreeOddGables|Three Odd Gables]]

A human child taken by the fey and raised into an elf. She brokers ruin for the
godmothers and wants to bring down every power that ever shaped her fate.

## Under the Mask

Slim build, angular features. Pointed ears, golden eyes with cat-like pupils. 5'3",
110 lbs. Her hair falls to the middle of her back and is usually braided. It looks black,
but in bright light it shines iridescent red, purple, or blue depending on the angle.

## The Mask

It looks like the face of a human skull, distorted by magic to fit as a mask. It has red
stripes that always look like fresh, wet blood but are dry to the touch. The stripe
pattern shifts with her mental state.

- **Where she got it**: She made it with help from the fae who raised her and taught her magic.
- **What it actually is**: Her **mother's skull**. Her wand is her mother's **fibula**, intricately carved.
- **What she keeps behind it**: Spite, resentment, and grand ambition. Hatred of authority and of being controlled.

## The Secret That Would Get Her Dusted

- She was a human child, "kidnapped" by a servant of one of the godmothers and raised by the fey until she became an elf.
- She was really her **mother's payment** for a godmother's favor.
- She was older than the fey's usual targets, so **she remembers** all of it.
- About 30 years after becoming an elf, **she killed her mother**.
- She secretly wants to overthrow **both the Duchess and the godmothers**, so that nobody but her can shape her destiny.

## The Godmothers

- She was raised in the Family and works for them.
- They charged her for raising her into an elf and teaching her magic, and they kept her from her freedom until they felt the debt was paid.
- **Her payment** was finding people who had something worth the hags' price and bringing those doomed people to them.
- She and the godmothers have been **"even" for a long time**. Now they pay her for her work, and she also takes a finder's fee from each client she introduces.

## The Masquerade

She has **never been** to the [[DHonaireEstate|Masquerade]]. The Family prefers that its
facilitators stay out of the spotlight.

## The Conspiracy

- **Why she's in**: Ambition and revenge. To control her own destiny, the system must fall or she must become the new Duchess. Either way, the current Duchess has to go.
- **If the Duchess finds out**: Probably execution or imprisonment.
- **Who brought her in**: One of her clients offered the information as payment for an introduction to the godmothers. Instead of her usual, more tangible fee, she took that information plus an introduction to the conspiracy.
- **Trust**: She doesn't trust that client at all, but she doesn't really trust anyone.

## GM Notes
- **Inspiration**: Her mask is a nod to Ichigo's hollow mask from *Bleach* (the player's own reference).
