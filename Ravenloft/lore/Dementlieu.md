# Dementlieu

> Source tags: **[VRGtR]** = Van Richten's Guide to Ravenloft (2021) · **[Classic]** = older Ravenloft lore, useful but not in the 5e book · **[Homebrew]** = my own ideas to fill gaps

## At a Glance [VRGtR]
- **Darklord:** Duchess [[npcs#Saidra d'Honaire|Saidra d'Honaire]]
- **Genre:** Dark fantasy / psychological horror. Think *Cinderella* plus *The Masque of the Red Death* plus *Dark City*.
- **Hallmarks:** Masquerades, decadent aristocracy, social decay, illusions, imposter syndrome
- **Population:** ~13,600, mostly humans with some halflings
- **Capital:** [[PortALucine|Port-a-Lucine]] (called Port-de-Lucine in older editions). The domain is basically one port city and not much else.
- **Waters:** Lucine Bay, Pernault Bay

## The Big Lie
Everyone in Dementlieu pretends to be richer than they are.
- Commoners act middle class and dig through rubbish at night for food and scraps.
- The middle class acts upper class and starves to pay for jewelry and gowns. Clothes get mended over and over.
- **Masks** from the balls are worn in daily life too, until a person's mask is as recognisable as their face.
- The countryside ([[Chateaufaux]]) is supposed to be where the noble estates and farms are. It isn't real. It never gets closer no matter how long you walk, and food doesn't actually come into the city from anywhere. **Nobody says this out loud.**
- Anyone who exposes the truth, or "fails to keep up appearances," disappears. At night the Red Death (Saidra) unmasks and disintegrates pretenders and anyone who reveals how things really are.

## Government [VRGtR]
- **Governor:** [[npcs#Marcel Guignol|Marcel Guignol]]
- **The Council:** Five aristocratic advisors, including Saidra, who is by far the most influential. They write the laws and command a small city watch.
- [Classic] In older lore this body is the **Council of Brilliance**, a "republic" whose elections the elite rig. Hellfire-club-style secret societies of the ultra-rich decide who runs.

## Religion
- **Ezra** is the main faith. The [[MotherOfTearsCathedral|Mother of Tears Cathedral]] treats every class as equal, but its clergy do nothing about the class divide. Mostly they weep over the world's horrors.
- [Classic] Dementlieu's church is a liberal branch of Ezraism. The poor believe. The rich just pay their tithes.

## The Grand Masquerade [VRGtR]
- Saidra holds a masquerade **every week** at the [[DHonaireEstate|d'Honaire Estate]]. Everyone in the domain wants an invitation.
- The book's intended structure: **the PCs arrive → work to get an invitation → attend the ball.** Something they need (a MacGuffin) is at the ball, and the book has tables for deciding what it is.
- Guests who break character, look out of place, or are exposed as frauds get killed. Gatecrashers are killed on the spot.

## Mists & Borders
- When Saidra closes the borders, the Mists swallow the edges of the city and the false countryside.
- [Classic] Mist visits are heavier and more frequent here than in most domains. Fog banks work as Mistways to wherever the darklord chooses. Locals lock their doors on **new moon** nights.

## Older Flavor Worth Stealing [Classic]
- Paris crossed with Venice, roughly 18th-century tech: canals as the main roads, spires and steeples everywhere, star-forts guarding the harbor.
- Barrier islands in the bay. Some are manicured manors for the Council. Others are "creepy, stinking tangles" that locals avoid.
- Corbeau-accented high culture: fashion, opera, art, and salons that people across the Core copy.
- **Dominic d'Honaire** was the darklord in older editions: a charming telepath who controlled minds. In 5e he's gone, but his name could still be around (see [[plotHooks]]).
