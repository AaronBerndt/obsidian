# The Darklord

### Saidra De'Honaire [VRGtR]
Duchess of Dementlieu. A **wraith** who uses makeup, gowns, and illusion to pass as a living noblewoman. At night she becomes **the Red Death**, a murderous spirit with a red glow that unmasks and disintegrates pretenders.

**Backstory (dark Cinderella):**
1. She grew up on a farm. Her father said he was an exiled duke, and her mother died when she was young.
2. She bullied the local children by insisting on a noble birthright she didn't have.
3. Her father remarried a wealthy, cruel woman with two cruel daughters, and they made Saidra their servant.
4. She learned the truth: her father had never been a duke. He was a duke's **servant**, dismissed for stealing silverware.
5. Heartbroken, she prayed at her mother's grave. A **kind, grandmotherly spirit** gave her a gown and jewels for a masquerade celebrating a new duke's coronation.
6. She went planning to murder the duke and take his title. Instead they fell for each other and danced until midnight.
7. At midnight a **plague** (the Red Death) struck the ball. The dying duke confessed that he was the silverware thief's son, kept as heir after the real ducal family was exiled. That made him **her own brother**.
8. Saidra stabbed him in a rage and soon died of the plague herself. She woke up as a wraith in Dementlieu.

**Torment:** She finally has the nobility she always craved, but as a wraith she can't taste the food, feel the silk, or enjoy any of it. She is terrified of being exposed and hates what she has become. She takes it out on anyone who "pretends" to a status they haven't earned, which is exactly what her whole domain does.

**DM notes [Homebrew]:**
- Who was the grandmotherly spirit? Probably the Dark Powers in disguise. She could show up again as a "fairy godmother" who offers the PCs invitations.
- What Saidra wants: to be loved for who she really is. What she fears: being seen as she really is. She can't have both.

# The Government

### Marcel Guignol [VRGtR]
Mayor of Dementlieu. On paper he runs the city. In practice the Council's five advisors, and Saidra above all, pull his strings. *Guignol* is French for a puppet show.

### The Council of Five [VRGtR / Homebrew names]
Five aristocrats advise the Governor, and Saidra is one of them. The book doesn't name the other four, so here are some to use.
- **Comte Lucien Varrault**: owns the docks and the city's only real food supply (smuggling through the Mists?). He's terrified someone will find out.
- **Madame Ysolde Pernault**: the family Pernault Bay is named after. A gossip queen whose salon decides who gets invited to the Masquerade.
- **Baron Thibault Morisse**: commands the city watch. A sincere believer in order who honestly thinks the disappearances are crime.
- **Vicomtesse Céleste Arnaud**: secretly broke. She's selling family heirlooms to the [[ThreeOddGables|hags]] to stay in the game.

# The Estate & Servants

### The Ghoul Staff [VRGtR]
The [[DHonaireEstate|d'Honaire Estate]] is staffed by **ghouls** in livery, dressed for the occasion. Guests pretend not to notice.

### House De'Honaire
Crest: Sun 

Her father, stepmother, and two stepsisters are all part of her past. [Homebrew] Did the Dark Powers bring them into the domain as torments or tools? Ideas:
- **The stepsisters, Ondine & Mirabelle**: two veiled ladies at every ball who never take off their masks. Under the masks are faces Saidra disfigured.
- **The father**: an old drunk at the docks who mutters about being a duke. Saidra can't bring herself to kill him.
- **The Duke/brother**: his ghost or revenant wanders the estate's halls, still wearing his coronation mask.

### Clergy of the Mother of Tears [VRGtR]
The priests of Ezra at the [[MotherOfTearsCathedral|cathedral]] weep constantly and offer comfort but nothing else.

### The Coven of Three Odd Gables [VRGtR]
Green hags at the edge of the city. They help poor folk "rise" into high society, and every deal has a cruel catch.

### The Red Widow's Performers [VRGtR]
Shapeshifters (rumoured to include **giant spiders**) work the [[RedWidowTheater|Red Widow Theater]] and lure patrons away.

### The Ash-men of La Cendrée [Homebrew]
Undead who sweep up the dust of the Red Death's and Duchess' victims
