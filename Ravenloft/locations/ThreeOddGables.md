# Three Odd Gables
Three crooked houses on the edge of [[PortALucine|Port-a-Lucine]], home to a small coven of **green hags**. They help poor people break into high society, and every bargain has a sinister catch. [VRGtR]

### The Look [Homebrew]
The only place in the city deco never touched: three crooked, old-world houses of black timber and moss, leaning on each other. The sleek towers around them seem to lean away. Inside, though, the hags sell pure deco glamour: gowns, masks, and gilt, laid out on rotting tables.

### Notes [Homebrew]
- They're a twisted mirror of Saidra's fairy godmother: gowns, glamours, and invitations, all for a price.
- Example prices: your reflection, your real name, your firstborn's face, or a midnight curfew. At midnight the glamour drops, and if anyone sees you as you really are, you belong to the coven.
- Names: **Mère Brune, Tante Grise, Little Lisette**. Lisette is new, and she may once have been one of their "clients."
- They know Saidra's secret and her weakness, and they'd trade it for something big.
