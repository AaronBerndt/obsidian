# The Last Thread
A clothing store in [[PortALucine|Port-a-Lucine]], run by [[CrimsonFall|Crimson Fall]]. [Homebrew]

### Notes
- Everyone in the city needs their clothes mended over and over, so everyone comes through eventually. Crimson sees who's fraying before they do.
- It's how she finds clients for the godmothers of [[ThreeOddGables|Three Odd Gables]]. The ones who can't afford the repair get offered a different kind of fix.
- The name works three ways: the last thread of a fraying coat, the people who walk in hanging by theirs, and the last stop before the Gables.
