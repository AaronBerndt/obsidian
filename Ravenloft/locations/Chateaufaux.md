# Chateaufaux
Supposedly the countryside full of aristocratic estates that feeds the city. In truth the Mists start right at the city's edge. There's no farmland, and nothing comes in. You can see the countryside, but it never gets any closer however long you walk. Everyone knows, and nobody says so. [VRGtR]

*(Chateaufaux ≈ "false château.")*

### The Look [Homebrew]
From the city's edge, Chateaufaux looks exactly like the travel posters in every hotel lobby: stylized rolling hills, geometric poplars, white villas, and a sunset in perfect bands of gold and coral. It's too perfect. It looks *painted*, because maybe it is.

### Notes [Homebrew]
- A great early horror beat: the party sets out for a "nearby" estate and walks for hours without getting anywhere.
- So where does the food come from? Comte Varrault's ships run through the Mists, the hags conjure it, or (worst case) it's something nobody should ask about.
- Nobles talk about "summering at our Chateaufaux estate." All of them are lying, and all of them know the others are lying too.
