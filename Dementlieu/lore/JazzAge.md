# Jazz Age Dementlieu
Tone layer for the campaign. **1920s art deco + The Great Gatsby + Streets of New Capenna**, laid over Saidra's [[Dementlieu]].
The pitch: *the party never ends, because the moment it does, everyone has to admit they're broke, starving, and dead inside.*

## How Far to Push the Tech
Pick a dial and stick with it:
- **Light touch:** Gaslight, horse-drawn carriages, gramophones. The 1920s shows up only in fashion, music, and attitude.
- **Full Jazz Age (recommended):** Electric marquees, phonographs, cameras with flash powder, a few motorcars that only the ultra-rich own. Nobody knows where the electricity comes from. (Lamordian imports through the Mists?)
- Firearms: stay with D&D weapons and reflavor them. A hand crossbow is a "pocket piece," and a heavy crossbow is a "chopper."

## The Look (Art Deco)
- **Architecture:** Stepped towers, sunburst motifs, chevrons, and gold leaf over black marble. Gargoyles on the [[DHonaireEstate|d'Honaire Estate]] get restyled as sleek gilded angels with blank faces.
- **The skyline:** Spires lit gold at night. From the bay the city looks like a jewelry box. Up close the gold leaf is peeling.
- **Masks:** Deco masks in lacquer, enamel, and feathers. Each one is a geometric "brand," and people talk about "the Silver Chevron" or "the Lacquered Crescent" instead of using names.
- **Fashion:** Beaded flapper dresses, long pearls, cigarette holders, cloche hats, pinstripes, spats, and tuxedos with masks. Everything is borrowed, pawned, or mended.
- **Light:** Neon-gold marquees against the grey Mist. The Mist glows with reflected light, but the street below stays dark.

## The Sound
- Jazz bands at every party, playing a little too fast, as if they're scared to stop. [Homebrew] *If the music at the Masquerade ever stops, the Red Death comes.*
- Torch singers at the [[RedWidowTheater|Red Widow]].
- Phonographs in empty townhouses play to rooms where nobody is listening.

## The Gatsby Layer
Saidra **is** Gatsby. She invented herself, she throws parties to be seen, and she's chasing a past she can't get back.

| Gatsby | Dementlieu |
|---|---|
| Gatsby's parties | The weekly Masquerade. Everyone comes, and nobody knows the host. |
| Gatsby reinventing himself | Saidra the farm girl became "Duchess d'Honaire" |
| Old money vs. new money | The Council's "old" families sneer at climbers, and they're all frauds too |
| The green light on Daisy's dock | **A green lantern** across the bay (see Julien Gastonne below) |
| The Valley of Ashes | **La Cendrée**: ash flats between the city and [[Chateaufaux]] |
| Eyes of Dr. T.J. Eckleburg | **The Watching Mask**: a giant faded advertisement over La Cendrée |
| "Rich girls don't marry poor boys" | Saidra's whole tragedy |
| Gatsby dead in his pool, no one at the funeral | What happens to every guest who's unmasked |

### La Cendrée (Valley of Ashes) [Homebrew]
- A grey wasteland at the city's edge where the **dust of the Red Death's victims** blows and settles. The poor live here.
- Ash-sweepers collect the dust. *What are they selling it as?* (See Lustre below.)
- **The Watching Mask:** an enormous painted billboard of a deco mask with hollow eyes, advertising a perfume company that went under decades ago. Locals swear the eyes move. Maybe the Dark Powers really are watching.

### Julien Gastonne, the Man Across the Bay [Homebrew NPC]
- A new-money millionaire who throws the second-best parties in town on his barrier-island mansion. Nobody knows where his money comes from (bootlegging Lustre?).
- Every night he stands on his dock looking at the d'Honaire Estate, where **a green lantern** burns.
- He's in love with Saidra, or with the girl she was. He may be the brother/Duke's soul given a new face, or just a man who came out of the Mists remembering a dance.
- He wants the PCs to arrange a "chance" meeting. It ends in tragedy either way: she can't let him see her, and he can't stop looking.

## The New Capenna Layer: Five Families
The Council of Five are really **crime families** in white gloves. Each has a racket, a style, and a mask motif. Saidra holds the fifth seat, and her family's racket is *the Masquerade itself*.

| Family | Head | Racket | Capenna Vibe | Mask Motif |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **House Varrault** | Comte Lucien Varrault | Docks, smuggling, the *food* | Riveteers / bootleggers | Iron anchor, riveted bronze |
| **House Pernault** | Madame Ysolde Pernault | Salons, parties, invitations, the [[RedWidowTheater\|Red Widow]] | Cabaretti | Peacock fan, rhinestones |
| **House Morisse** | Baron Thibault Morisse | The city watch, contracts, "protection" | Brokers | Silver scales, a sealed wax seal |
| **House Arnaud** | Vicomtesse Céleste Arnaud | Art, auctions, forgery, discreet killings | Maestros | Porcelain, a painted tear |
| **The Veiled Seat** | Duchess Saidra d'Honaire | Secrets, blackmail, the Masquerade | Obscura | A blank black oval |

- **The Veiled Seat** [Homebrew]: Saidra never comes to Council meetings in daylight. Her chair holds a blank black mask on a stand, and her voice comes out of it. The other families assume it's a trick of sorcery. Nobody has ever checked whether anyone is behind it.
- Families trade favors in **markers**: gilded chips stamped with the house sigil. Whoever holds enough markers gets a Masquerade invitation.
- Prohibition: the Council banned liquor "for the public good," which made speakeasies the best racket in town. Every family runs one.

### Lustre (the Halo analog) [Homebrew]
- A shimmering gold powder sold in tiny deco compacts. Dust it on your face and for one night you *look* rich: radiant skin, perfect posture, a voice like old money. Nobody can tell you're a pretender.
- Mechanically: advantage on Deception/Persuasion to pass as noble until midnight, then disadvantage on Wisdom saves until a long rest. Addictive.
- **The secret:** it's made from the ash of people the Red Death turned to dust. Their stolen dignity, worn by the next pretender.
- Every family wants to control the supply. Saidra doesn't know it exists. *If she found out, what would she do?*

## Speakeasies & Haunts [Homebrew]
- **The Gilded Cage:** Pernault speakeasy behind a birdcage shop. Password: "*I'm here for the canary.*"
- **Le Dernier Verre (The Last Glass):** a Varrault dock bar inside a sunken ship's hull, below the waterline.
- **The Arnaud Gallery:** an auction house where paintings sell for fortunes. Some sales are contracts on a life, with the "title" of the painting as the target.
- **Morisse Notary:** where you sign contracts with the watch. The ink is red, and the fine print is binding.

## Slang to Sprinkle In
- **"Gilded"**: rich, or pretending well. "She's gilded."
- **"Tarnished"**: exposed as poor. "Poor sap got tarnished."
- **"Dusted"**: killed by the Red Death.
- **"Bare-faced"**: unmasked; a terrible insult.
- **"Taking the long walk"**: heading for Chateaufaux, a euphemism for suicide or madness.
- **"Midnight money"**: cash that disappears when the illusion breaks.
- **"Going green"**: pining for something you can't have (from Julien's lantern).
- **"The bee's knees," "the cat's pajamas," "applesauce!"**: the classics, used a little too eagerly.
