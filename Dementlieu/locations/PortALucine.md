# Port-a-Lucine
The capital, and pretty much the whole domain. A port city on Lucine Bay that looks gorgeous and is quietly starving. [VRGtR]

### Notable Locations [VRGtR]
- [[DHonaireEstate|d'Honaire Estate]]: Saidra's manor and home of the weekly Masquerade
- [[MotherOfTearsCathedral|Mother of Tears Cathedral]]: Ezra's weeping church
- [[RedWidowTheater|Red Widow Theater]]: a garish cabaret with a giant red spider on the roof
- [[ThreeOddGables|Three Odd Gables]]: a hag coven on the edge of town
- [[Chateaufaux]]: the "countryside" that isn't there

### Districts [Homebrew, inspired by Classic]
- **Le Haut (The Heights):** Townhouses with gilded facades. Behind the front rooms, half of them are empty, with the furniture sold off.
- **The Canals:** Gondolas are the main transport, and the boatmen hear everything. Something swims in the canals at night.
- **Quayside / Theater Row:** Opera houses, cabarets, and the [[RedWidowTheater|Red Widow]]. The Phantom's old haunt.
- **The Docks (Pernault Wharf):** Ships come in, but from where? Dock workers sing to the sea on moonless nights.
- **Les Ruelles (The Alleys):** Where the "middle class" goes after dark to scavenge, masked so no one recognizes them.
- **The Barrier Islands:** Council manors on some. Stinking tangles that nobody visits on others.
