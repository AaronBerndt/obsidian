# d'Honaire Estate
Saidra's lair, and the most lavishly decorated estate in Dementlieu. Leering gargoyles and lewd tapestries everywhere. **Ghoul servants** in livery wait on the guests. [VRGtR]

### The Weekly Masquerade [VRGtR]
- Held every week. An invitation is the most valuable thing in the city.
- If a guest breaks character, looks out of place, or turns out to be a fraud, Saidra destroys them.
- Gatecrashers are killed immediately.
- The book has tables for the MacGuffin the party is after at the ball.

### Rooms & Set Pieces [Homebrew]
- **The Grand Ballroom:** Walls of mirrors. Saidra has no reflection, so she stands where the mirrors are draped in black.
- **The Banquet Hall:** A beautiful feast that Saidra never touches. A sharp guest notices.
- **The Clock Gallery:** Dozens of clocks, all stopped at **midnight**.
- **The Duke's Room:** Locked and untouched since the coronation ball. A bloodstained dagger, a coronation mask, and maybe the brother's ghost.
- **The Wardrobe:** Hundreds of gowns. One is the original from the fairy godmother, and it may be a weakness or a key to her undoing.
- **The Servants' Stair:** Where the ghouls feed. The smell is covered with perfume.

### Masquerade MacGuffin Ideas [Homebrew]
1. A letter proving the Duke was the silverware thief's son
2. The mask of someone a PC lost to the Mists
3. A key to the Mistway out of Dementlieu
4. Saidra's mother's locket
5. A guest list that is really a death list for tonight
6. A Council member who wants to defect and be smuggled out
