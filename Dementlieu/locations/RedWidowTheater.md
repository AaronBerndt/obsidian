# Red Widow Theater
A loud, garish cabaret known for provocative dancing and shady dealings. A **giant red spider statue** sits on the roof. Rumor says shapeshifters lure patrons away, and some of them may be **giant spiders** that eat customers. [VRGtR]

### Notes [Homebrew]
- The one place where the rich and poor mix, all masked and all lying.
- The owner, **Madame Arachne Vey**, deals in secrets. She can get the PCs into the Masquerade, or sell them out.
- A dancer hasn't come back from a "private performance" in the cellar, which is a web-filled lair.
- [Classic] The **Phantom** may be working the theater district.
