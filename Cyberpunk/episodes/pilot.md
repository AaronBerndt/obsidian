# Episode Pilot: **Vitrine**

> Recover a stolen medical cabinet before dawn. There's a person inside it.
>
> One session, ~3.5–4 hrs. Clock runs 22:40 → 04:00.

---

## Characters

| PC | Hook in | Button to press |
| -- | ------- | --------------- |
| [[Bell]] | Hired muscle on a straightforward recovery job | **Always spare the innocent.** The cargo is a woman. Kane shows up wearing a badge. |
| [[Ada]] | Cheap, available, and nobody else would take it | **Del Okoye** is a working Netrunner on the other side. Burn her, take her programs. |
| [[Salvadore]] | Only person who can keep the cabinet's occupant alive | **Every person is a canvas.** Someone already treated this one as one. |
| [[Sebastian]] | Opens on stage; his show is the crew's alibi | The manifest names a **Reyes** subsidiary. |

**Opening question, one line each**: why do the other three trust you enough to be in a
car with you at 2 a.m.?

---

## The Gig

- **Fixer**: **Sparrow** (Ines Vaquero) — Watson fixer who moves medical and pharma.
- **Client**: **Hollis Vane**, a name three fixers use for the same account at
  **Solace Vitality**, a Westbrook longevity clinic.
- **Pay**: **2,000eb** split (500 each), **+1,500eb** if delivered sealed. Sal keeps
  the cabinet's supply drawer.
- **The Job**: At 21:15 a **Vector Protection** van was boosted on the Watson ramp.
  Two guards dead. Cargo was one coffin-sized **medical cryocabinet** on internal power.
  Get it back **unopened** to a Heywood garage by **04:00**.
- **What Sparrow knows**: the thieves are the **Slick Six**, a small Watson boosting
  crew who thought they were hitting combat drugs. She does not know what's in the box.

### The Twist — three layers, in this order

1. **It's a life-support unit.** 40-amp draw, coolant cartridge every six hours. The
   Slick Six have it on a stolen generator. It's already on its second cartridge.
2. **There's a living woman inside.** Early twenties. No cyberware, no dental work, no
   scars, no prints on file. A fresh surgical **neuroport** at the base of her skull.
   She's a **blank** — a host body prepped for a personality transfer.
3. **The buyer is family.** Solace Vitality is held by **[REYES FAMILY COMPANY]**.
   The transfer client is an 81-year-old board member. The pilot only needs to *plant*
   this.

> Sebastian's sheet leaves the family company unnamed. Suggested: **Reyes Biotics**.

## Scenes

### 1 — Strong Start: The Gutterline *(20 min, 22:40)*

Open mid-song. Sebastian is nine bars into the second chorus on a dead metro platform
under Kabuki. Bell works the stairwell. Sal is stitching a ganger's eyebrow in the
alcove. Ada is holding down the local NCPD band so the show can finish.

**Vector Protection** — not NCPD — comes down the east stairs sweeping lights. They're
running a grid search for the stolen van. Give everyone one action to turn the panic
into an exit. Nobody has to fight; Vector disengages fast.

Sparrow's call lands while they're still in the service tunnel.

### 2 — The Meet: [[BrightAisle|Bright Aisle]] *(15 min, 23:20)*

A 24-hour pharmacy on the Kabuki edge of Watson — six minutes' walk from the Gutterline,
so they arrive still sweating. Fluorescent, everything behind mesh, a clerk who is never
the same person twice. Sparrow takes meets in the stockroom at a folding card table,
boxed in by pallets of expired inventory she buys by the ton and resells by the dose.
Paper ledger open in front of her. **No chrome hits the table** — that's her one rule,
and she'll wait while you empty your hands.

She gives them the job, the clock, and the word **unopened**, which she repeats twice
without explaining. Pushed (Persuasion DV 17), she gives up the name **Vane**. She won't
go further — she doesn't know more, and she's annoyed about it.

### 3 — The Ramp: reading the crime scene *(25 min, 23:45)*

**Optional but strongly recommended.** Sparrow can tell them *who* took it. Only the
hijack site can tell them *what* they're carrying — and every hour they learn it early
is an hour the back half of the session gets to breathe.

The eastbound Watson on-ramp. Vector swept it, NCPD tagged it and left, and the van sits
under a weighted tarp waiting for the **05:00 tow**. One bored contract guard on a
folding chair with a thermos. Talk past him (Persuasion/Bribery DV 13 — he's paid until
five and does not care), badge him, distract him, or wait out the break he takes anyway.

**What the scene says about the coffin:**

- **Arc scoring and a cut cable** at the van's cargo circuit. The cabinet was **plugged
  into the van** and drawing power the whole trip — someone cut it live, in a hurry.
  Cargo doesn't need power. *(Basic Tech / Electronics DV 13)*
- **A torn coolant cartridge wrapper** in the gutter, twenty feet out. They swapped a
  cartridge **at the roadside**, fast, without gloves. So the unit was already alarming
  when they took it — and they had a **spare**, meaning they figured out what they were
  holding within ten minutes. *(Sal, automatic)*
- **Four caster gouges and drag marks.** It went out on a pallet jack, not carried.
  Call it 400kg. Whatever hauled it away has a lift gate. *(Perception DV 13)*
- **A condensate trail** — sixty feet of it, evaporating. It's cold, and it's still
  **running**. *(Perception DV 15, or free for Sal)*
- **Blood that isn't the guards'** on the door frame. Arterial, from a hand, and the
  wound pattern is *punching through laminate with an unarmored borg limb*. Whoever did
  this is badly hurt and hasn't gone to a hospital. *(Sal, First Aid/Medicine DV 13)*
- **Two blood pools**, bodies gone — Trauma Team logged and hauled at 21:41.

**The logs** — the van's event recorder is still in the wreck. Electronics/Security Tech
DV 13, or Ada jacks straight in:

- **Power log**: a sustained **40-amp draw** on the cargo circuit for the entire route.
  This is the single biggest tell. *Nobody runs 40 amps to a cooler.*
- **90 seconds of cabin audio**: impact, glass, a man screaming about his hand, and a
  young woman's voice going fast and furious — *"You said it was drugs. You said it was
  DRUGS."* Then a pallet jack. Then thirty seconds of a soft two-tone alarm nobody in
  the recording recognizes. **That's the low-coolant warning.**

**Where this lands them**: if they work the ramp, they arrive at the Petal Room in Scene
5 already knowing there's a life-support unit on a dying clock — and possibly already
guessing there's someone in it. **That's the good version of this session.** Let them
have it.

> **If they skip the ramp entirely**, nothing breaks. All of the above is still
> available from Sal in Scene 4 and from the cabinet itself in Scene 6 — they just meet
> it cold, under pressure, with Vector already knocking.

### 4 — Legwork *(30 min, 00:15–01:30)*

Three avenues. Any two find the location; **all three means they know what's inside**
before they get there, which changes the back half. Let them split up.

- **Street** *(Sebastian, Bell)* — Streetwise DV 15, Bribery DV 13, or a call to
  **Lemmy** → the Slick Six pushed a crate into **the Petal Room**, a shuttered nail
  salon on Charter with a ripper suite in the basement.
- **Net** *(Ada)* — see architecture below. The Vector dispatch node holds the manifest.
- **Body** *(Salvadore)* — Cybertech/Medicine DV 15 on the model number: it's a
  **Vitrine-class transport unit**. Organs don't ship in those. Whole bodies do. Sal is
  now the only one who can read the clock.

> **If they stall**: at 01:00 Mink calls Sparrow trying to give the thing back. The
> address arrives free.

### 5 — The Petal Room *(50 min, 01:30)*

Dead salon on Charter, Watson. Pink tile, dry foot baths, a sign still promising gel
nails for 12eb. Stairs down to a two-room ripper suite. Generator in the alley, cable
through a broken window. The cabinet hums in the back room.

Inside: Mink, Dozer, two boosters, and **Mari Quist**, drunk, who already popped the
inspection panel.

**This is not a fight unless the crew makes it one.** The Slick Six are terrified and
Mink has been hunting a buyer for four hours.

- **Front, talking** — Persuasion DV 15. Mink *wants* an exit.
- **Back, quiet** — Stealth DV 15 past the generator. **Cutting the generator kills the
  cabinet.** Only Sal will know that.
- **The Net** — Ada loops cameras and pops the basement door from across the street.

**Pressure**: Dozer won't let anyone near Mink, and he escalates on any move toward her.

### 6 — The Reveal *(20 min, 02:15)*

Frost on the viewport, then a face. Read it slow. **She's awake** — sedated, but her
eyes track.

**Sal's read** (any Medicine/Paramedic success): ~25, muscle tone of someone who's never
walked a full day, no chrome, no fillings, no childhood scars. Neuroport seated within
two weeks. Bio-tag etched inside the left forearm: **SV-LOT-12**. Stable for another
**90 minutes** on this cartridge, then she cooks.

**If they open it**, she can speak. Vocabulary fine, memory empty. No name. She asks,
in order: *"Is it time?"* → *"Am I supposed to be someone yet?"* → *"Please don't put
me back."*

Point Sal and Bell at this and get out of the way.

### 7 — The Squeeze *(25 min, 02:45)*

Two things inside five minutes:

- **Sparrow calls.** Client raised the offer — *if it's still sealed.* *"Tell me you
  didn't open it."*
- **Vector arrives.** Four operatives and **Y Reyner**, who tracked the coolant
  signature or followed Ada's trace. Reyner **knocks**. Her offer: hand it over, walk
  away, no bodies, no report, **800eb** out of her own budget. She will not discuss the
  woman as a person and looks genuinely confused if you try.

**Optional — Kane** *(cut if long)*: NCPD rolls the block. Kane sees Bell, holds eye
contact, drives on. No dice. Just the look.

> **Running long?** Cut Kane, and cut the Vector operatives down to two. Reyner alone
> is the scene.

### 8 — The Call *(20 min, 03:15–04:00)*

Three endings. Don't steer.

| Choice | Gets | Costs |
| ------ | ---- | ----- |
| **Deliver her** | Full pay, sealed bonus if genuinely unopened. Sparrow's pleased; a door opens with Vane. | Sal and Bell both know what they did — **ask them out loud**. Solace files them as reliable, so the next call is worse. |
| **Run her** | Nothing. Sparrow eats the loss and remembers. | Vector opens a recovery file on all four. But they have **her**, and she's now a character. |
| **Sell her elsewhere** | 1,200–2,500eb from a rival clinic, a Nomad pack, or Wraith Reyes if you want to swing hard. | Sparrow *and* Vector angry, and the buyer is worse than Solace. |

End on the same image either way: sky over the Hot Zone going red at 04:00, cabinet
full or empty.

  tunnel east.

## Secrets and Clues

Give these out wherever they dig — never gate one behind a single check.

- [ ] It's a **Vitrine-class whole-body transport unit**. Organs don't ship in those.
- [ ] 40 amps, one coolant cartridge per six hours. It's on its second.
- [ ] The van's power log shows the cabinet drew **40 amps for the whole route** — it
      was plugged in and running, not shipped as freight.
- [ ] They swapped a cartridge **at the roadside**, which means they had a spare, which
      means they knew what they had within ten minutes of taking it.
- [ ] The cabin audio has a young woman shouting *"You said it was DRUGS"* over a
      two-tone alarm — the unit's low-coolant warning.
- [ ] There's a **living woman** inside, sedated but conscious.
- [ ] **No chrome, no dental work, no scars, no prints** — she's never been a person on
      paper.
- [ ] The **neuroport** is two weeks old and cost more than the crew is being paid.
- [ ] Bio-tag reads **SV-LOT-12**. There are at least eleven others.
- [ ] The manifest lists **Solace Vitality**, held by **[REYES FAMILY COMPANY]**.
- [ ] An **"integration procedure"** is booked for **06:00** — it was happening this
      morning.
- [ ] **Hollis Vane** isn't a person; it's a shared account name.
- [ ] The Slick Six killed the guards **by accident** — Dozer panicked. Mink's been
      trying to give the box back for hours. *(Bell's "spare the innocent" gets
      genuinely murky here.)*

## Opposition

CP RED is lethal. Say it out loud before Scene 5.

Handgun/Evasion +10 · Medium Pistol 2d6. *Surrender at half HP or if Dozer drops.*

**Dozer** (lieutenant) — `REF 6 · BODY 8 · HP 45 · Light Armorjack SP11`
Brawling +14 · Big Knucks 2d6+2, Wolvers 3d6 if pushed. *Borged arms, hair-trigger,
will not let anyone near Mink.*

**Vector Recovery Operative** ×4 — `REF 7 · BODY 7 · HP 35 · Medium Armorjack SP12`
Shoulder Arms +13 · Heavy SMG 3d6 (Assault Rifle 5d6 if the crew shot first) ·
flashbangs · Low-Light cybereyes. *Fight in pairs, suppressive fire, disengage on
Reyner's word.*

fight the crew should win by shooting.

## NPCs

**Sparrow (Ines Vaquero)** — *Fixer. Watson. Medical and pharma routing.*
Forties, tired, chain-vapes, keeps a paper ledger because she doesn't trust the Net.
Deals honestly and hates surprises. **Wants**: Solace to keep calling — it's the
steadiest money since the war. **Truth**: she didn't know, and she isn't going to ask
next time either.
> *"I don't open boxes. That's not modesty, that's the job description."*

**Mink (Amara Voss)** — *19. Runs the Slick Six.*
Small, fast, bleached buzzcut, grease under acrylic nails. Went independent too early.
**Wants**: to hand this to anyone and go home.
> *"We were told combat drugs. Who fills a coffin with drugs?"*
*If she lives → a young, indebted fixer-in-training the crew can call later.*

**Dozer** — *22. Slick Six muscle.*
Borged arms, no neck, loyal past the point of usefulness. Killed both guards in four
seconds and hasn't said a word about it. Stands between the crew and Mink saying *"nah"*
until someone makes him move.

**Mari Quist** — *Ripperdoc, Petal Room basement.*
Late forties, steady hands, half a bottle in, struck off a real hospital. Popped the
panel two hours ago and has been sitting on the stairs since. **She says "blank" out
loud.** Sal's foil — same training, opposite ending.
> *"I've stripped chrome off corpses. First time I've closed a lid."*

**Y Reyner** — *Vector Protection, Recovery Lead.*
Forties, corporate haircut, trained voice, immaculate manners. Treats the crew as
contractors on the wrong side of a paperwork problem. **Wants**: asset recovered,
no report filed.
> *"I'm authorized for eight hundred and a clean walk. I'm also authorized for the
> other thing. I'd rather do the eight hundred."*
*The campaign's recurring antagonist, if they leave her alive and impressed.*

**SV-LOT-12 — "Twelve"** — *The blank.*
Early twenties, unmarked, perfect in a way that's deeply wrong. Speaks clearly, thinks
slowly, has no past. Not childlike — **unwritten**. If the crew keeps her she's a real
character: she picks her own name in the first downtime.
> *"They said someone was coming to live in me. Is it you?"*

**Del Okoye** — *Vector's contract Netrunner. Westbrook.*
Competent, bored, freelance, no loyalty past the invoice. **Burn her** and Ada gets
three programs plus a reputation for burning Netrunners — exactly what she's trying to
escape. *Serve that irony straight.* **Talk to her** and she sells the manifest for
300eb, no hard feelings.

**Hollis Vane** — *The client. Never appears.*
A voice on a phone and a hand truck at a garage door. A face to go looking for in
Episode 2 and not find.

**Lemmy** — *Rocker, NCPD circles. Sebastian's tech, Bell's hobby buddy.*
Free information. Knows which blocks Vector is sweeping and that a Slick Six kid was
asking about "freezer buyers" at midnight. Then asks if they want to hear his new
track. Make them listen.

**Kane** — *Solo, NCPD, Bell's childhood enemy.* *(Cameo, cut freely.)*
Thirty seconds in Scene 6. Sees Bell, holds eye contact, drives on. The point is that
he's still in the city, still has a badge, and chose not to act *tonight*.

**Gosetsu** — *Ada's mentor.* *(Optional, remote.)*
Pings her mid-run, unprompted: *"Floor five is a Raven. Whatever you take out of there,
they'll know your address by breakfast."* Offers no help. Wants to see if she goes in
anyway.

## Loot & Payout

- **2,000eb** on delivery, **+1,500eb** if genuinely unopened. Sparrow won't check;
  Vane's man will.
- **Sal** — 2 doses of Speedheal, a sealed coolant cartridge, and a **Vitrine service
  key** worth more than both.
- **Ada** — Sword, Armor, Hellhound if she burns Del. Del's contact code if she doesn't,
  which is arguably better.
- **Sebastian** — the manifest. If he puts it in the show, he starts a war with his
  family an episode early. **Let him.**
- **Bell** — nothing material. Sparrow's respect, or its withdrawal.
- **Reyner's 800eb** if they take the clean walk.

**Humanity**: nothing here costs Humanity in points. It costs it in the answer to
*"what did you do with her?"* — say that answer out loud at the table.

## Heat

- **Vector Protection** — Reyner opens a recovery file. Patient, funded, shows up in
  Episode 3, not 2.
- **Solace / [REYES FAMILY COMPANY]** — an 81-year-old board member missed a 06:00
  procedure. Someone senior is now personally inconvenienced. **This is the campaign's
  spine.**
- **Sparrow** — more Solace work if they delivered; a grudge she mentions every single
  time if they didn't.
- **Kane** — saw Bell on a street with two dead guards on it, and wanted her to know.
- **The Slick Six** — Mink alive means a crew that owes them. Mink dead means Watson
  hears they work for corporate recovery money.
- **The other eleven lots** — SV-LOT-12 implies 1 through 11. Eleven more people in a
  Westbrook basement without names.
