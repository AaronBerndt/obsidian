# Episode 1: **Ninety Minutes**

> Carries [[pilot|Episode Pilot: Vitrine]] Scenes 5–8 forward and supersedes them.
>
> One session, ~3.75 hrs. Clock runs **01:30 → 04:00**.
>
> **Where we left off**: the crew worked the ramp. They know the coffin is a running
> life-support unit on a coolant clock, and they're most of the way to guessing there's
> a person in it. They are parked on Charter Street looking at a dead nail salon.

## The Gig

- **Fixer**: **Sparrow** (Ines Vaquero), out of [[BrightAisle|Bright Aisle]].
- **Pay**: unchanged — **2,000eb** split, **+1,500eb** if it arrives sealed. Heywood
  garage, **04:00**, Vane's man with a hand truck.
- **The Job**: get the cabinet out of the `Petal Room` basement and across town before
  the clock runs out. Sparrow does not know about the battery. Sparrow does not know
  about the woman. **Sparrow is still saying the word "unopened."**

### The clocks

**Three pressures, and they hand off to each other.** Only one is ever live at a time,
so the table always knows what it's racing. Times in scene headings are **in-fiction**;
the minutes are table minutes.

1. **Coolant — 01:30 to 03:00.** The cartridge has **~90 minutes** left. This is the
   clock for Scenes 1–4: *get to the cabinet.* **Say this number out loud in Scene 1.**
2. **Battery — 11 minutes, once they unplug it.** The cabinet draws **40 amps** off the
   alley generator; **cutting it kills her** in about twenty minutes of thermal mass.
   *(They don't know the battery spec yet — Sal finds it in Scene 2.)*
3. **Money — 04:00.** Vane's man at the Heywood garage. Hard stop.

> **The handoff**: they should reach the cabinet at ~02:55, about five minutes before the
> coolant runs out. That's when [[Salvadore]]'s **sealed spare cartridge** from the pilot
> pays off — it buys six hours and takes coolant off the board entirely. Coolant stops
> being the problem the moment Sal solves it, **and the 11-minute battery becomes the
> problem instead.** If Sal doesn't have the spare, they're carrying a dying box.

> **The design in one line**: getting in is easy. Getting 400kg of powered coffin up a
> flight of stairs and onto another power source in eleven minutes is the hard part.
> **Exfil is the boss fight**, and Vector arrives during it.

## Scenes

### 1 — The Van *(15 min, 01:30)*

Cold open, parked on Charter with the lights off, four people and the smell of somebody's
cold noodles. Across the street: papered windows, a dead sign promising gel nails for
12eb, and a generator note-thrumming somewhere behind the building.

**Open on Sal doing the math out loud.** Ninety minutes. Then let the table sit in it.

Ninety seconds later the front door opens and a kid in a puffer jacket steps out to smoke.
Nineteen, maybe. He doesn't look around once. **First look at the opposition, and the
first hint that this is not a hardened target — it's five scared kids and a drunk.**

**Ask the question that frames the session**: *what are we actually doing here — taking
the box, or taking her?* Don't let them answer yet. Let it sit.

### 2 — Case It *(45 min, 01:45)*


Four vantage points. **Each one gives a different PC something only they can get**, so
split the party and rotate fast. See [[PetalRoom|the Petal Room]] for the floorplan.

**The laundromat roof, across Charter** — [[Bell]]. Perception DV 13 / Tactics DV 15.
→ **The timings.** `Teeth` (front step, smokes every ~20 min, clockwork). `Rat` (upstairs
window, rifle across his knees, on his phone the entire time — DV 9 to get past him,
he is not looking). `Dozer` at the top of the basement stairs, moving only for the
bathroom every ~40 min. `Mink` cycling out to the alley for signal.

**The alley** — [[Salvadore]]. Basic Tech DV 13, or free if he gets hands on the unit.
→ The generator, the 40A run, the cable through the broken window — and, stencilled on
the cabinet's service plate: **`INT. BATT — 11 MIN @ LOAD`**.
**★ This is the session's key fact and Sal is the one who finds it.** Give him the beat.

**The Net** — [[Ada]]. Thin, cheap, unguarded — let her have the easy win:
```
LOBBY  Password DV 6
 1  Control Node DV 8   → front lock + camera loop
 2  File DV 10          → ★ Mari's appointment book
```
The appointment book gives the real basement layout — *and* eleven prior entries billed
to the same Solace account, going back four months. **The other lots have come through
this room.**

**The street** — [[Sebastian]]. Streetwise DV 13 / Persuasion DV 13.
→ The laundromat owner, who has watched that building for nine years and will talk for
free: the **storm drain access** in the alley that comes up inside the back room, the
roll-up door that "hasn't opened since the salon closed until tonight," and that the girl
with the acrylic nails has been outside on her phone since ten, and she's been crying.

### 3 — The Plan *(20 min, 02:15)*

**Sit back. This is the players' scene.** Do not nitpick, do not poke holes, do not ask
them to roll for the plan. Let them commit to something.

Ask exactly one question, and only when they think they're finished: ***"What's your
out?"*** — because the exfil is the part they'll have skipped.

**One mechanical hook**: [[Bell]] rolls **Tactics DV 15**. On a success, the crew banks
one **"we anticipated that"** — at any point later in the session they may retroactively
declare one piece of prep they'd made (a cut lock, a van repositioned, a spare cable, a
lookout posted). One use. No other subsystem needed.

**The four ways in**, for reference:

| Way | Check | Cost |
| --- | ----- | ---- |
| **Front door** | Persuasion DV 15 — `Mink` genuinely wants an exit | Everyone in the building knows you're there |
| **Alley roll-up** | Pick Lock DV 13 (Sebastian, 4) or bolt cutters | Loud. It's a roll-up door. |
| **Storm drain** | Athletics DV 13, tight and filthy | Comes up **inside Room B**, at the cabinet. Nobody expects it. |
| **The Net** | Ada, already done | Pops the front lock, loops cameras, can kill the basement lights |

> **The trap to let them walk into**: cutting the generator is the obvious way to kill the
> lights and the cameras at once. **It also kills her.** Sal knows. Nobody else does
> unless he says so. If a player proposes it and Sal's player doesn't catch it — let them
> get all the way to the switch before Sal gets a roll.

### 4 — Execute *(50 min, 02:35)*

The plan works for about ninety seconds. Then roll or pick from this:

| d6 | Complication |
| -- | ------------ |
| 1 | **Dozer moves early.** No bathroom this cycle — he's coming down the stairs. |
| 2 | **Mari wakes up** on the stairs, drunk and loud, and starts *talking to them.* |
| 3 | **The generator surges.** The cabinet alarms — that two-tone tone from the ramp audio — and everyone in the building hears it. |
| 4 | **`Rat` actually looks out the window.** Once. It's enough. |
| 5 | **`Mink`'s buyer calls back** and says yes. She has ten minutes to hand it to someone who isn't the crew. |
| 6 | **A sixth Slick Six kid comes back with food**, through the door the crew came in. |

**Keep the pilot's framing**: this is not a fight unless the crew makes it one. `Mink` has
been trying to give this thing away for four hours. `Dozer` is the problem — he will not
let anyone near her, and he escalates on any move in her direction.

**If it does go loud**: `Mari` hits the floor and stays there. `Teeth` and `Rat` surrender
at half HP or the moment `Dozer` drops. `Mink` does not run.

### 5 — The Lid *(20 min, 02:55)*

They already suspect. **The reveal isn't that there's a person in it — it's that she's
awake and she can talk.**

Frost on the viewport, then a face. Eyes that track. Sal's read, free at this point:
~25, muscle tone of someone who's never walked a full day, no chrome, no fillings, no
childhood scars, a two-week-old surgical **neuroport**, and a bio-tag inside the left
forearm reading **`SV-LOT-12`**.

She speaks. Vocabulary fine, memory empty, no name. In order:
*"Is it time?"* → *"Am I supposed to be someone yet?"* → *"Please don't put me back."*

**`Mari` is the one who says the word.** *"It's a blank. You know what a blank is? Neither
did I, till Tuesday."* Then, to Sal, because she can tell he's the one who'll understand:
*"I've stripped chrome off corpses. First time I've closed a lid."*

Give [[Salvadore]] and [[Bell]] the room. Everyone else waits.

> **The clock handoff, at ~03:00.** The coolant cartridge runs out *during this scene* —
> the two-tone alarm from the ramp audio, in the room, with her watching them hear it.
> **Sal's sealed spare** buys six hours and takes coolant off the board. If he doesn't
> have it, `Mari` does — one, in the cabinet under her surgical sink, and she will hand
> it over for nothing. **From here on, the only clock is the battery.**

### 6 — Eleven Minutes *(30 min, 03:15)*

**The set piece.** They unplug it. Start counting.

400kg on four casters. Up a flight of basement stairs that was built for a nail salon.
Through a back hall, out a roll-up door, into an alley, onto whatever they brought.

**Run this in real rounds.** Eleven minutes is 110 rounds, which sounds like a lot until
they try to get a pallet jack up stairs.

- **The stairs** are the wall. Athletics/Brawling DV 15 as a crew action, or a plan that
  doesn't involve the stairs at all — *the storm drain is wide enough for the cabinet,
  which is how it got down there.* Reward anyone who worked that out in Scene 3.
- **Power at the other end** — do they have an inverter? A lift gate? A vehicle at all?
  If Scene 3 skipped the exfil, **this is where that bill comes due**, and the answer is
  usually "Sal's clinic, six blocks, and we are going to find out how fast this van goes."
- **Every 3 minutes**, a status beat: the internal fan changing pitch, the viewport
  fogging, her hand against the glass.
- **If the battery runs out**: she doesn't die instantly. She has about twenty minutes of
  thermal mass, and then it's Sal, a First Aid kit, and a DV 17 that he should not make.
  **Don't fudge it. Let the clock be real.**

### 7 — The Squeeze *(25 min, 03:22 — mid-exfil, 4 minutes of battery left)*

**Vector arrives mid-exfil** — in the alley, cabinet on a hand truck, battery under four
minutes. Perfect worst timing. Four operatives and **Y Reyner**, who tracked the coolant
signature or followed Ada's trace.

**Reyner does not kick anything. She knocks on the side of the cabinet.**

Her offer, flat and professional: hand it over, walk away, no bodies, no report, **800eb**
out of her own recovery budget. She will not discuss the woman as a person, and she looks
**genuinely confused** if anyone tries.

> *"I'm authorized for eight hundred and a clean walk. I'm also authorized for the other
> thing. I'd rather do the eight hundred — it's less paperwork for both of us."*

**And Sparrow calls**, at the worst possible moment: *"Tell me you didn't open it."*

**Optional — Kane** *(cut if running long)*: NCPD rolls the block. He sees [[Bell]], holds
eye contact, drives on. No dice. Just the look.

> **Running long?** Cut Kane, cut two operatives. **Reyner alone is the scene.**

### 8 — The Call *(20 min, 03:45–04:00)*

| Choice | Gets | Costs |
| ------ | ---- | ----- |
| **Deliver her** | Full pay, sealed bonus if genuinely unopened. Sparrow's pleased; a door opens with Vane. | Sal and Bell both know what they did — **ask them out loud, at the table**. Solace files them as reliable, so the next call is worse. |
| **Run her** | Nothing. Sparrow eats the loss and remembers it every time. | Vector opens a recovery file on all four. But they have **her**, and she's a character now. |
| **Sell her elsewhere** | 1,200–2,500eb from a rival clinic, a Nomad pack, or **Wraith Reyes** if you want to swing hard. | Sparrow *and* Vector angry, and the buyer is worse than Solace. |

End on the same image either way: the sky over the Hot Zone going red at 04:00, and the
cabinet either full or empty.
