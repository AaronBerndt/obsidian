# NPCs

## Fixers

The crew's job pipeline. Who calls them, what cut they take, what they want in return.

- **Cortez** — [[Bell]]'s former boss, who remembers her fondly. Runs with an
  edgerunning crew.
- **Sparrow** *(Ines Vaquero)* — Watson fixer who moves medical and pharma. Brokered the
  `Vitrine` job. Chain-vapes, keeps a **paper ledger** because she doesn't trust the Net,
  and holds court in the stockroom of [[BrightAisle|Bright Aisle]]. Deals honestly and
  hates surprises. *"I don't open boxes. That's not modesty, that's the job description."*
- **Hollis Vane** — not a person. A name at least three fixers use for the same
  **Solace Vitality** account. Never appears; sends a man with a hand truck.
- **Unnamed local Fixers** — they send [[Ada]] her netrunning clients.

## Corporations

Who is hiring, who is being robbed, and who is at war with whom.

- **The Reyes family company** *(unnamed on the sheet)* — the Corporate Exec family
  [[Sebastian]] was raised in. His life goal is to take it down. **Solace Vitality is
  one of its portfolio holdings** — the campaign's first hard link to it.
- **Solace Vitality** — Westbrook longevity clinic. Buys **blanks** (unwritten host
  bodies) and performs full personality transfers into them. At least twelve lots to
  date. Held by the Reyes company. *The spine of the campaign.*
- **Vector Protection Group** — private security contractor. Hauls Solace's freight and
  recovers it when it goes missing. Professional, well-funded, not sadistic. See
  **Y Reyner** (Law).

## Gangs

Turf, colors, and what they'll kill over.

- **Local gangers** — [[Salvadore]]'s main clients; they protect his work area and home
  in exchange for medical help.
- **The Slick Six** — a small Watson chrome-boosting crew, all of them under 23. Boosted
  the Vector van in [[pilot|Vitrine]] thinking it was combat drugs.
  - **Mink** *(Amara Voss)*, 19 — runs them. Bleached buzzcut, grease under acrylic
    nails. Boosted cars for a fixer at sixteen and went independent too early. Wants out.
    *If she survives, a young fixer-in-training who owes the crew.*
  - **Dozer**, 22 — muscle. Borged arms, loyal past the point of usefulness. **Killed
    two Vector guards by accident** and hasn't said a word about it. Right hand wrecked
    and going septic.
  - **Teeth** and **Rat** — the other two. Nineteen, terrified, surrender easily.

## Law

NCPD, Max-Tac, Trauma Team, private security, and anyone who can put a bounty out.

- See **Kane** (Enemies) and **Lemmy** (Media & The Street) — both move in
  NCPD-employee circles. [[Bell]] is also wanted by the law; maybe she did it, maybe
  she didn't.
- **Yolanda "Y" Reyner** — Recovery Lead, **Vector Protection Group**. Corporate haircut,
  trained voice, immaculate manners. Treats edgerunners as contractors on the wrong side
  of a paperwork problem — solvable, not personal. Opens every encounter by talking and
  means it; kills without heat and stops the instant the objective is secure. **Will not
  discuss a blank as a person**, and looks genuinely confused if you try.
  *"I'm authorized for eight hundred and a clean walk. I'm also authorized for the other
  thing."* **The campaign's recurring antagonist if she's left alive and impressed.**

## Fixers' Regulars

Ripperdocs, techs, netrunners-for-hire, dealers, bartenders, and the medtech who
doesn't ask questions.

- **Gosetsu** — [[Ada]]'s teacher or mentor.
- **Gibbs** — [[Salvadore]]'s mentor.
- **Marisol "Mari" Quist** — ripperdoc, cuts four nights a week in the basement of
  [[PetalRoom|the Petal Room]]. Late forties, steady hands, struck off a real hospital.
  Won't work on anyone she thinks is under sixteen — the only rule she's never broken.
  **[[Salvadore]]'s foil**: same training, opposite ending. She's the one who says the
  word *blank* out loud. *"I've stripped chrome off corpses. First time I've closed a lid."*
- **Del Okoye** — Vector's contract Netrunner, out of a Westbrook conapt. Runs Sword,
  Armor, and Hellhound. Bored, freelance, no loyalty past the invoice — she'll sell the
  same data she's defending for 300eb. **A live option for [[Ada]]'s lifepath** (steal
  programs off Netrunners you brain-burn), with the irony that burning her *builds* the
  bad reputation Ada is trying to escape.
- **Eve** — [[Salvadore]]'s former lover and friend. *(His tragic-love-affair table
  also lists her as having died in an accident.)*
- **Gabriella** — [[Bell]]'s lover. Died, either via accident or murder.
- **Yuki Cooper** — [[Sebastian]]'s lover. Mysteriously vanished; still an open wound.

## Media & The Street

Screamsheet reporters, rockerboys, braindance editors, and anyone who moves a story.

- **Lemmy** — Rocker who moves in NCPD-employee circles. [[Sebastian]]'s music tech,
  and [[Bell]]'s hobby buddy — they geek out together.

## Enemies

Standing threats. Who is actively working against the crew, and why.

- **Wraith Reyes** — [[Sebastian]]'s brother, who betrayed him. Fixer; Corporate
  Ladder Climber.
- **Kane** — an enemy from [[Bell]]'s childhood. Solo; NCPD employee.
- **Lily** — [[Salvadore]]'s ex-lover. Can throw "just themselves" at him; his planned
  revenge is to backstab her indirectly. *(His tragic-love-affair table also lists her
  as having died in an accident.)*
- **Unknown figure** *(name not filled in on the sheet)* — a mysterious Fixer in
  government-employee circles working against [[Bell]]. She doesn't even know they exist.
- **A Fixer who sees [[Bell]] as a threat** — from her Solo lifepath.
- **A Fixer or client who wants [[Ada]] exclusively** — gunning for her over her
  netrunning services.
- **Corporate enemies of [[Sebastian]]** — they don't like his message and target his
  guerrilla performances.

## Wildcards

People who don't fit anywhere else yet, and whose column depends on what the crew does.

- **"Twelve"** *(bio-tag `SV-LOT-12`)* — a **blank**. Early twenties, no cyberware, no
  dental work, no scars, no prints on file, and a two-week-old surgical neuroport at the
  base of her skull. Grown or stored as a host body for a Solace personality transfer
  that was scheduled for 06:00 and didn't happen. Speaks clearly, thinks slowly, has no
  past — **not childlike, unwritten**. *"They said someone was coming to live in me.
  Is it you?"*
  - **If the crew kept her**: she picks her own name in the first downtime, and she is a
    character, not a MacGuffin.
  - **If they handed her over**: she is somebody else now, and that somebody sits on a
    board.
  - **Either way**: the tag implies **lots 1 through 11**, and **Mari Quist**'s
    appointment book at [[PetalRoom|the Petal Room]] proves they came through.
    Eleven people, somewhere, without names.
