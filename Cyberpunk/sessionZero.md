# Session Zero — Cyberpunk RED

## Pitch

One paragraph: what kind of campaign is this? Street-level edgerunners scraping by,
corporate deniable assets, nomad convoy work, or a Night City power climb?

-

## Tone Dials

- **Grit** — how lethal? (Cyberpunk RED is lethal by default; say so out loud.)
- **Chrome** — how much cyberware is normal, and how hard do we push Humanity Loss?
- **Hope** — is the ending "burn out bright" or "get out alive"?
- **Lines & Veils** — what's off the table, what happens off-screen.

## Setting Baseline

- **Year**: 2045, the Time of the Red
- **City**: Night City — rebuilt in pieces, run by no one, owned by everyone with a gun
- **Net**: shattered. No global Net. Local architectures only, and the DataKrash left
  rogue AIs and demons in the black wall's shadow.
- **Money**: eurodollars (eb). Nobody is rich. Everybody is one bad month from the street.
