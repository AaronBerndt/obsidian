# Bell

**Role**: Solo (Local Vigilante for Hire)
**Source**: [Demiplane character sheet](https://app.demiplane.com/nexus/cyberpunkred/character-sheet/68a6d0db-8e2f-44ad-833a-e9663fe5a0ac)

A Combat Zone kid who raised herself on the street and came out of it stable, serious,
and hard — Neo-Militarist gear, a mohawk hung with decorations, and a body built for
close work. She hires out as a vigilante wherever the money goes, but she won't touch
the innocent, and she's wanted by the law for something she may or may not have done.
Honor is the thing she values; fixing an old mistake is the thing she's chasing.
