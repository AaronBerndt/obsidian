# Ada

**Role**: Netrunner
**Source**: [Demiplane character sheet](https://app.demiplane.com/nexus/cyberpunkred/character-sheet/07ab544d-432a-4258-a81e-3699b0c8857d)

Arrogant, proud, and aloof — an Edgerunner family's kid raised behind Corp Zone walls
until betrayal took everything, and now the most valued person in her life is herself.
She runs out of a living space her rig has completely swallowed, steals her programs off
the Netrunners she brain-burns, and wants one thing: to get out from under a bad
reputation.

## Notes

- **This sheet is the least finished of the four.** Eight of ten STATs are blank (only
  INT 7 and TECH 7 are set), so HP, Humanity, and every derived value are uncomputable.
  Interface rank is also missing. Not playable as-is.
- **No Interface skill and no Language skill are listed**, despite six languages appearing
  in the cultural-origins block. The language field holds the complete East Asian menu
  from the lifepath table rather than a picked language — most likely never chosen.
- **The campaign notes in [[characters]] agree with this sheet on every point.** The
  Netrunner entry reads "Goal: To fix their rep" (sheet: "Get rid of a bad reputation"),
  "Only cares about themself" (sheet: most valued person is *Yourself*; people are
  "obstacles to be destroyed"), "Friends: Mentor" (sheet: **Gosetsu**, a teacher or
  mentor), and "Family Lost everything" (sheet: lost everything through betrayal). The
  notes list no enemy, and neither does the sheet.
- **Internal tension worth resolving:** she values **Love** most, but her most valued
  person is *herself* and she treats people as obstacles to destroy. Could be deliberate
  characterization — someone who wants love and is bad at it — or a leftover roll.
- **Second tension:** the family background is *Edgerunners*, but the childhood
  environment is a *safe Corp Zone walled off from the rest of the City*. Edgerunner
  parents raising a kid behind corporate walls needs an explanation, and "lost everything
  through betrayal" may well be it.
- Gosetsu does not appear anywhere in [[characters]] yet — the notes only say "Mentor."
