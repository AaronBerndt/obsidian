# Gig: **The Last Cut**

> Play the tribute set at a dead rockerboy's wake. While the room watches
> [[Sebastian]], lift six reels of master tape out of the host's archive.
>
> One session, ~3 hrs. Self-contained. Clock runs **20:00 → 00:30**.
>
> **No Solace, no Vector, no Reyes.** This is the job the crew took *instead*.

---

## Where This Sits

They delivered her. Full pay, sealed bonus, a door open with Vane, and Sparrow calling
more than she used to — which is the problem. Solace has them filed as **reliable**.

So when **Cortez** calls [[Bell]] for the first time in two years and asks for *her crew*
rather than for her, they take it, and everyone at the table knows why. Nobody has to
say it. **Don't make them say it.**

> **One optional beat, thirty seconds, cuttable**: at the wake there is a woman in good
> shoes wearing a **Solace Vitality** lanyard she forgot to take off. She is not plot.
> She does not look at anyone. Put her in [[Salvadore]]'s eyeline once, then never again.

## Characters

| PC | Hook in | Button to press |
| -- | ------- | --------------- |
| [[Sebastian]] | **Torch Mbeki was his.** He learned three chords off *Ninth Ward* before he learned his family was worth hating. He's been asked to play the tribute. | He is playing a memorial hosted by the man who ordered the killing. **He owns the clock.** |
| [[Salvadore]] | Nobody else can dress four people to pass in Westbrook, and nobody else can read what's in the casket. | **Every person is a canvas.** The reconstruction on Torch's temple is *beautiful*, and Sal knows whose hands did it. |

**Opening question, one line each**: what's the one thing you spent the Vitrine money on?

## The Gig

- **Fixer**: **Cortez** — [[Bell]]'s former boss. Takes meetings from the back booth at
  **Ochoa's**, an all-night counter in Heywood. Straight, sentimental, and cheap.
- **Client**: **Nadia Vey**, Torch Mbeki's producer and, twenty years ago, his partner.
  She says the masters are hers by contract. She has the contract. It's real.
- **Pay**: **3,200eb** split (800 each), **+1,000eb** if the reels arrive **cold and
  undamaged**. Nadia pays in cash out of a gig bag, which tells you what she sold to
  fund it.
- **The Job**: **Corben Vaal** is hosting Torch's wake on the 38th floor of the Glass
  Bell tower, Westbrook. Two floors up, in a climate-locked archive, sit **six reels of
  analog master tape** — Torch's entire unreleased catalogue, bought off the estate
  nine days after he died. Get them out during the party.
- **What Cortez knows**: the layout, the guest list, and that Vaal's security lead is
  **Ilse Prynne** and is very good. He does not know what's on reel six.

### The Twist — three layers, in this order

1. **Reel six isn't a song.** It's a session tape cut **three nights before Torch died**.
   Twenty minutes of a scared forty-four-year-old man talking to a room, and about
   nine minutes in he says a name. The name is **Corben Vaal**.
2. **Vaal had him killed for the catalogue.** A dead artist's back catalogue is worth
   three to five times a live one, and Vaal's business is buying the last of things.
   The appraisal on Vaal's node is dated **twelve days ago** and values the Mbeki
   catalogue *"in the event of the artist's death."* He commissioned it before there
   was a death to have an event about.
3. **The cover-up was surgical, and Sal knows the hands.** The body in the open casket
   has a post-mortem reconstruction of the left temple — blunt force, dressed out to
   look like nothing. Under the hairline is a **three-point rosette suture**, a forty-
   minute technique almost nobody bothers with anymore. **Gibbs** taught it to Sal.

> Vaal gives a toast at 22:45 about what Torch's second record meant to him, and **he
> means every word of it, and he cries.** Play it completely straight. He loved the
> work. He just wanted to own it, and the work is worth more when the artist is done
> making it.

### The clocks

**One hard clock, one soft one.** Say both out loud in Scene 3.

1. **The set — 40 minutes.** [[Sebastian]] plays a scheduled forty-minute tribute set at
   **23:00**. The floor is full and the archive is empty for exactly as long as he's on
   stage. At **23:40** Vaal takes three guests upstairs to see the collection. That is
   the whole gig.
2. **The tape — soft, ~90 minutes.** Out of the climate case, above 8°C, acetate starts
   to go. It doesn't stop them, it costs them the **1,000eb** bonus and possibly the
   nine minutes on reel six. **Sal can solve this.** See Loot.
3. **The load-out — 23:45.** The band's gear goes down the freight elevator. It is the
   only route out of the building that isn't a lobby with a guest book.

> **The design in one line**: getting in is a costume problem, getting into the archive
> is a key problem, and getting **14kg of tape out of a Westbrook tower** is the actual
> gig. The case fits in a guitar flight case. **Let them work that out themselves.**

## Scenes

### 1 — Ochoa's *(20 min, 20:00)*

An all-night counter in Heywood. Six stools, a griddle, two booths in the back, and a
window that hasn't been clean since the war. Open on the register: somebody has taped a
**screamsheet obituary** to it — a quarter page, badly printed, Torch Mbeki at maybe
thirty in a photo somebody took at a show. Next to it is a paper cup with **forty-one
eurodollars** in it, collecting for a funeral that has already happened.

Two hours from now, forty floors up in Westbrook, a hundred and forty people are going
to eat catered food off silver at the same man's wake. **Put that image down now and
don't mention it again.**

**Cortez** is in the back booth with a coffee he isn't drinking. He is genuinely glad to
see [[Bell]] and genuinely bad at showing it. He gives the job, the pay, and the room:

- Vaal wants a name on stage. Nadia got [[Sebastian]] onto the shortlist and Vaal said
  yes because a Reyes kid playing a Watson wake is *exactly* the kind of thing Vaal
  collects.
- The reels are on 39. The guest list is on Vaal's node. The security lead is Prynne
  and Cortez says her name the way you say a weather warning.
- **Nadia is in the next booth** and comes over when called. Pushed on *why now*
  (Persuasion DV 15 / Human Perception DV 13), she says the contract expires in six
  weeks and Vaal knows it. Pushed harder (DV 17), she admits she doesn't want the
  catalogue. **She wants reel six**, and she won't say what's on it, because she isn't
  sure and she's afraid of being right.

> **Ochoa's is Cortez's room, not a fixer bar.** No house rules, no neutral ground, no
> drinks named after dead legends. Nobody holds court here — a tired man eats here every
> night and takes meetings at the same table because it's the table he's already at.
> The crew will keep coming back to this booth; give the place a cook and a name for the
> cook the first time they do.

### 2 — Legwork *(30 min, 20:40–22:00)*

Four avenues. **Each one is somebody's** — split them up and rotate fast. Any two get
them in the door; all four means they walk in already knowing about reel six, which is
the good version of this session.

**The look** — [[Salvadore]]. Personal Grooming / Wardrobe and Style DV 13.
→ Four people who pass in a Westbrook room. This is not a joke check: Sal decides who
each of them *is* tonight, and the table should say those names out loud. He can also
buy the crew one free re-roll on any Persuasion check on 38 by having done the work.
**Bell will hate every second of this. Let her.**

**The paper** — [[Ada]]. Forgery DV 13 off the node's staff manifest (see architecture).
→ Two guest invites and two catering credentials. The credentials are the useful ones.
**Her name is the one on the forged manifest line.** Say that out loud once.

**The street** — [[Sebastian]]. Streetwise DV 13, Bribery DV 13, or a call to **Lemmy**.
→ Torch's last session was booked at a Watson room called Hollow Sound for three nights,
paid in cash, engineer sent home. Nobody has seen the tape. Also: Torch had stopped
drinking eleven months ago and everybody in Watson knows it, and the ruling was an
overdose, and **nobody has said that out loud in the same sentence yet.**

**The building** — [[Bell]]. Perception DV 13 / Tactics DV 15 from the plaza or the
service dock across the street.
→ Four guards in soft jackets, not a hardened post. Freight bay on the north face, load
in at 21:00 and load out at 23:45, one bored dock man with a clipboard. The 39th floor's
windows are dark and stay dark. **And the catering van has six people getting out of it,
one of whom is a kid who cannot be older than fifteen.**

### 3 — The Plan *(20 min, 22:10)*

**Sit back. Players' scene.** Don't poke holes, don't ask for a roll on the plan.

State the two numbers plainly before they start: ***forty minutes of set, and the case
weighs fourteen kilos.*** Then ask exactly one question, once, when they think they're
done: ***"How does it leave the building?"***

**One mechanical hook — the setlist.** [[Sebastian]] writes down **eight songs** now.
One song = five minutes = the whole clock. During Scene 5 he may **extend** — Persuasion
DV 15 (or Charismatic Impact if you're tracking it) — and each success adds a song and
**+1 Attention**. See Scene 5. This is the only subsystem the session needs.

**The four ways into the archive:**

| Way | Check | Cost |
| --- | ----- | ---- |
| **Vaal's physical key** | Pick Pocket DV 17 in a crowded room, or Persuasion DV 17 to get walked up early | Blows if it fails. He notices a missing key inside ten minutes. |
| **The service riser** | [[Salvadore]]'s **Vitrine service key** from `Vitrine` opens the cold-chain maintenance panel · Basic Tech DV 13 | A 40cm crawl. Nobody in armor, nobody with BODY 7+. **The case comes out this way.** |
| **The maglock, hard** | [[Ada]] Control Node DV 10 **and** Electronics/Security Tech DV 15 on the physical override | Writes a line to a log. **Prynne reads logs at load-out.** |
| **The catering lift** | Bribery DV 13, or a credential and a straight face | Gets you to 39's back hall. Does not open the archive. Still need one of the above. |

> **The good beat to protect**: the archive's climate unit is a repurposed medical
> cold-chain box, because that is what money buys when money wants tape kept at 4°C.
> **Sal's key from the pilot opens it.** If a player realises this themselves, give them
> the whole scene and say nothing.

### 4 — The Wake *(30 min, 22:30)*

Floor 38. Grey stone, low light, a hundred and forty people who mostly did not know him
and eleven who did. An open bar nobody is using properly. Torch's guitar on a stand
where a photograph should be.

**The casket is open at the north end**, and it is very good work.

**[[Salvadore]]'s read** — give him this in three beats, no roll needed for the first:

- *Free*: this is a **reconstruction**, not a touch-up. Left temple and jaw rebuilt.
  An overdose does not require that.
- *Medicine / Paramedic DV 15*: under the filler the bone is depressed. Blunt force,
  once, hard. And the tissue was held **cold for four to six days** before it was
  dressed — this body waited somewhere while somebody decided what to do about it.
- *Free, and no roll ever*: a **three-point rosette suture** under the hairline.
  **Gibbs.** His mentor stood over this body for forty minutes and did the best work of
  his life on it, and took the money.

**Meanwhile on the floor:**

- **Prynne** finds the crew within ten minutes, one at a time, and is *pleasant*. She
  compliments Sal's jacket and means it. She asks [[Bell]] which agency she's with and
  accepts the answer. **She is counting them.**
- **Vaal's toast at 22:45.** Two minutes, unpolished, about hearing *Ninth Ward* at
  nineteen. He cries. Nobody in the room thinks it's a performance, **because it isn't.**
- **Tobi**, the fifteen-year-old busser, drops a tray near [[Bell]] and apologises about
  six times more than is warranted. He is terrified of losing this job.

### 5 — The Set *(45 min, 23:00)*

**The set piece.** Sebastian goes on. **Start the clock and put it where everyone can
see it: 8 songs, 5 minutes each, 40 minutes.**

Cut between the stage and the crew every song or two. This should feel like a split
screen. Sebastian's player is not idle — they're spending the clock.

**Extending**: Persuasion DV 15 between songs. Success = +1 song (+5 min), **+1 Attention**.
Failure = the room's energy dips, no extension, and **+1 Attention anyway.**

| Attention | What changes |
| --------- | ------------ |
| 1 | Prynne moves to where she can see the stage. Nothing else. |
| 2 | Prynne notices she can't see two of the crew. She starts walking. |
| 3 | **Prynne stops watching the room and starts watching the band.** One guard is sent to check 39. |
| 4 | Vaal moves the walkthrough up. The archive has company in ten minutes. |

**Complications during the set** — roll or pick, once per two songs:

| d6 | Complication |
| -- | ------------ |
| 1 | **The riser is occupied.** A building maintenance drone is parked in the crawl and has to be moved by hand, and it logs the move. |
| 2 | **Tobi is in the back hall on 39** hiding from his supervisor, and he sees somebody he shouldn't. He does not raise the alarm. He just looks. |
| 3 | **A guest wants to go up.** Vaal, delighted, offers a private look at the collection to a woman he's been charming. Twelve minutes early. |
| 4 | **The case is locked to the shelf** — a shelf clamp, keyed to the same physical key as the door. Basic Tech DV 15, or leave with a shelf. |
| 5 | **Nadia is on the floor.** She said she wouldn't come. She came, she's drinking, and she's forty feet from Vaal. |
| 6 | **A song goes wrong.** Sebastian's rig cuts out mid-song. Lemmy can fix it in ninety seconds and the room goes quiet and everybody looks at the stage — which is either a disaster or exactly what the crew needed. **Ask them which.** |

> **If it goes loud**: it shouldn't, and Prynne will not draw a weapon in a room with a
> hundred and forty civilians in it. Neither should the crew. This is the fight the crew
> wins by being somewhere else. If a PC shoots first in that room, **that is the night's
> answer to who they became after the Vitrine job**, and you should let it stand.

### 6 — The Walkthrough *(25 min, 23:40)*

Vaal comes up with three guests and Prynne behind him. Whatever state the archive is in
is the state he finds it in.

**Three ways this scene can go, and all three are good:**

- **They're already gone.** Then the scene is the **load-out** at 23:45 — Lemmy rolling
  flight cases into the freight elevator, one of which weighs fourteen kilos more than
  it did coming in, and Prynne standing at the dock with a clipboard. Persuasion DV 15,
  or Bell's read on which case she'll actually open, or Sebastian coming down sweating
  and shaking hands and being *impossible to walk past*. If the maglock log got written,
  add DV +2 and she opens two cases instead of one.
- **They're still inside.** The riser, the dark, and 25 feet of archive between them and
  the panel while four people discuss provenance. Stealth DV 15, or a distraction that
  costs something.
- **They get caught.** Then it's a conversation, and Vaal is *fascinating* at it. He
  offers to buy the reels back at **6,000eb** and a signed thing he calls a courtesy
  agreement, and he is entirely sincere, and he will honour it, and the tape will never
  be heard again by anyone.

**The Prynne lever.** If anyone shows Prynne the **appraisal** — the one dated twelve
days before the death — she reads it twice. She does not help them steal anything. She
walks to Vaal, in front of his guests, hands him her building key, and quits. Then she
tells the crew, evenly, that she is off the clock and that they have about four minutes.

> She is not Reyner. Reyner won't discuss a person as a person and is confused when you
> try. **Prynne can be told something and change.** Play the difference deliberately;
> it's the whole reason this gig isn't a Vector job.

**The Tobi beat, and don't skip it.** The moment the theft is known, the default
corporate move is to hold the catering staff in the service hall until the client
decides who to blame. If nobody planned for it, **that happens**, and Tobi is fifteen,
and the crew will hear about it from Lemmy on the drive home. [[Bell]]'s moral compass
is on the sheet in writing. Give it a bill.

### 7 — The Call *(20 min, 00:05–00:30)*

Nadia is waiting in a car under the tower with a gig bag full of cash and no idea what
she's about to hear.

| Choice | Gets | Costs |
| ------ | ---- | ----- |
| **Give Nadia the reels** | Full pay, cold bonus if they kept them cold. She takes them home. **Ask her what she'll do with reel six and she won't answer.** | Nothing tonight. In three weeks she either breaks it or she doesn't, and the crew doesn't get a vote. |
| **Keep reel six, give her the rest** | Nadia pays anyway, minus the bonus, and is quietly relieved. [[Sebastian]] has nine minutes of a dead man naming his killer. | It's his now. Which means it's his problem, and Vaal collects. |
| **Put it in the show** | Sebastian plays reel six at a guerrilla set inside a week. Watson loses its mind. Torch gets a second funeral, a real one. | War with Vaal, opened by the crew, in public, with a Reyes name attached to it. **Let him.** |
| **Sell to Vaal** | **6,000eb**, more than the job, in cash, tonight. | Cortez finds out — Cortez always finds out — and Nadia sold something to fund a bag of money she now doesn't get to spend. |

End on the same image either way: the freight dock, the load-out finished, Lemmy asking
if anybody wants to hear the new mix, and forty floors up a window on 39 that's still dark.

## Opposition

CP RED is lethal. Say it out loud before Scene 5 — and then say the other thing out
loud too: **there are a hundred and forty civilians on floor 38, and nobody in this
gig wants a gunfight, including the opposition.**

**Reliquary Guard ×4** — `REF 6 · BODY 6 · HP 30 · Light Armorjack SP11 (under a suit)`
Handgun +12 · Medium Pistol 2d6 · Subdermal comms.
*Soft posture. Will not draw on the party floor under any circumstances. On 39 or in the
freight bay they will, and two of them is already a problem.*

**Ilse Prynne** (boss) — `REF 8 · BODY 7 · WILL 8 · HP 40 · Medium Armorjack SP12`
Handgun +15 · Heavy Pistol 3d6 · Combat Awareness 3 · Kerenzikov · Human Perception +14.
**The thing that will actually kill a PC**, and the thing most likely never to try.
Forties, ex-corporate close protection, does the job properly and goes home. Opens by
talking. **Can be told something true and act on it.** If the crew beat her cleanly and
without bodies, she remembers that with something close to warmth.

**Corben Vaal** — non-combatant. `BODY 3.`
Sixties, immaculate, genuinely knowledgeable, genuinely moved by the music. Had a man
killed the way you'd approve a purchase order. **Never raises his voice in this gig.**
Litigious long before he is violent — and then he is patient about that too.

**Gibbs** — non-combatant, and not present at the wake.
Sal's mentor. Took the job because it was the best work anyone had offered him in a
decade and because the money was very good. Will not lie to Sal if Sal asks him
directly. *"You'd have taken it. You'd have done the jaw better."*

## NPCs

**Cortez** — *Fixer. [[Bell]]'s former boss.*
Fifties, wide, slow-moving, eats at **Ochoa's** every night and has a tab there he has
never once settled in cash. Ran an edgerunning crew that Bell was the youngest
member of. **Wants**: to see whether she came out of the last two years as somebody he'd
still work with. **Truth**: he called her because Nadia asked for someone honest, and
Bell was the first name he thought of, and he was surprised by that.
> *"I asked for your crew. That's a promotion, kid, whether you like the sound of it or not."*

**Nadia Vey** — *Producer. Torch's partner, twenty years ago.*
Late forties, sharp, exhausted, sold a car to pay for this. She has the contract and the
contract is good, which means she could have gone to a lawyer and didn't, which means
she doesn't actually want the catalogue.
> *"I don't want his songs. I've heard his songs. I want the last twenty minutes of
> him talking, and I want to be wrong about it."*

**Corben Vaal** — *Collector. The host. The client's opposite number.*
Buys the last of things — final pressings, one-off masters, sole surviving prints.
Sixties, warm, unhurried, and an actual expert. He can talk about the second record for
an hour and it will be the best conversation [[Sebastian]] has had in a year.
> *"I own the only copy of eleven things in this city. Every one of them would be dust
> if I hadn't. You're welcome to think about that however you like."*

**Ilse Prynne** — *Security lead, the Reliquary.*
Forties, soft jacket, does not touch her weapon, does not raise her voice, counts the
room every four minutes without appearing to. Thought she worked for a rich man with
good taste. **Wants**: a clean night. **Truth**: she has never asked where anything in
that archive came from, and she is about to find out what that cost.
> *"You're the third person tonight who's told me they're with an agency. Two of you
> named the same one. I'm going to let that go, because it's a funeral."*

**Tobi Ferran** — *Fifteen. Busser, catering crew.*
Lied about his age to get on the van. Apologises constantly. Will be the first person
held when something goes missing, and the last person anyone thinks about.
[[Bell]]'s sheet says *always spare the innocent.* **This is the innocent.**

**Gibbs** — *[[Salvadore]]'s mentor. Bodysculptor.*
Sixties now, hands still steady, hasn't had real work in years. Rebuilt Torch's temple
and jaw in a cold room over two days and did it beautifully. Won't lie to Sal. Won't
apologise either.
> *"Somebody was going to close that face. I'm glad it was me. Look at it — look at it
> properly, and then tell me you're ashamed of me."*

**Lemmy** — *Rocker. [[Sebastian]]'s tech, [[Bell]]'s hobby buddy.*
On the load-in list, on the load-out list, on the stage-left riser the entire night with
his hands on the crew's exfil route. Free information, zero discretion, total loyalty.
Will absolutely chat to Prynne for eleven minutes about nothing. **Both a liability and
the plan.** Then asks if they want to hear the new mix. Make them listen.

**Torch Mbeki** — *Rockerboy. 44. Nine days dead.*
Four records, no money, the last honest voice in Watson. Eleven months sober. Everyone
in that room on 38 owns *Ninth Ward* and about eleven of them ever met him.

## Loot & Payout

- **3,200eb** on delivery (800 each), **+1,000eb** if the reels are **cold and
  undamaged**. Nadia checks. She'll know within thirty seconds of threading one.
- **[[Salvadore]]** — two sealed **Vitrine-compatible coolant cartridges** off the
  archive's cold-chain unit, which is both the loot and the solution to the soft clock
