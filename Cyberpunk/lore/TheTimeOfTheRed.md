# The Time of the Red

The years after the Fourth Corporate War. The sky still runs red at the horizon from
the dust the war kicked up, and it gave the era its name.

## What Broke

- **The Corps** — Arasaka and Militech gutted each other. The megacorps survive, but
  they no longer own everything outright. That gap is where edgerunners make a living.
- **The Net** — the DataKrash tore the global Net apart. What's left is local
  architectures behind the Blackwall, and whatever is on the other side of it wants in.
- **The Cities** — Night City took a nuke. Other cities took worse. Infrastructure is
  patchwork, and most of it is somebody's private property.
- **The Middle Class** — gone. There is corporate and there is street.

## What's Left

- **Fixers** run the economy that matters. Everything moves through a middleman.
- **Nomads** run the roads, and therefore the supply chain.
- **Medias** run the truth. Screamsheets are printed because the Net can't be trusted.
- **Solos** run everything else, briefly.
