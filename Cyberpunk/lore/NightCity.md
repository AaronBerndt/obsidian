# Night City, 2045

Rebuilt out of the rubble of the Fourth Corporate War. The nuke that hit the city
center left a crater the survivors filled in and called it good — the ruins are still
radioactive, and people still live in them.

No single power runs the city. The corps are weaker than they were, the NCPD only
polices where it's paid to, and the gangs hold the rest.

## Districts

Fill these in as the campaign touches them.

### City Center
The Hot Zone. Ash, rebar, and squatters. Nobody official goes in.

### Watson
Immigrant districts and open markets. Cheap chrome, cheaper rent.

### Heywood
Working class and gang-heavy. Home turf for a lot of edgerunners.

### Westbrook
Money. Clubs, corpo apartments, and the people who can afford to look clean.

### Santo Domingo
Industrial. Factories, power plants, and the people who keep them running.

### Pacifica
Abandoned resort development. Failed, flooded, and claimed.

### The Badlands
Outside the walls. Nomad packs, convoy routes, and nothing that stays put.

## The Rules of the Street

- Style over substance.
- Attitude is everything.
- Always take it to the edge.
- Break the rules.
