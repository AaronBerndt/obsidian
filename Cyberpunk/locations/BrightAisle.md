# Bright Aisle

A 24-hour pharmacy on the Kabuki edge of Watson. Everything worth stealing is behind
mesh, the fluorescents buzz at a frequency you feel in your teeth, and the clerk at the
counter is never the same person twice. The front is real — people do come here at
4 a.m. for antibiotics and painkillers. The business is in the back.

- **District**: Watson, Kabuki edge. Six minutes on foot from the Gutterline.
- **Who runs it**: **Sparrow** (Ines Vaquero) holds the lease through two shell names.
  The counter clerks are rotating and disposable, and none of them know who she is.
- **Who deals here**: Ripperdocs restocking off-book. Medtechs buying by the pallet.
  Gangers who need a course of antibiotics and no questions. Fixers who move pharma and
  want a neutral room with a legitimate reason to have a loading door.
- **House rules**: **No chrome on the table.** Sparrow will wait, pleasantly, while you
  empty your hands. Second rule: nothing is discussed in the retail space, ever — if
  you start talking business in front of the counter, the meeting is over.
- **What the crew can get here**: pharmaceuticals at a discount and dubious expiry
  dates, medical consumables by the case, a clean room to meet in, and Sparrow's
  paper ledger — which she does not show anyone, and which is the only honest record
  of who owes whom in three Watson blocks.

## Notes

- The stockroom is a folding card table surrounded by pallets stacked to the ceiling.
  Two ways out: the retail floor and the loading door onto the alley.
- Sparrow does not trust the Net and keeps everything on paper. That ledger is a
  target for anyone who wants leverage over half of Watson's medical trade.
