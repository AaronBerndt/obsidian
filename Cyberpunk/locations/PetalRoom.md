# The Petal Room

A dead nail salon on Charter Street, Watson. The windows are papered over with
newsprint, the sign still promises gel nails for 12eb, and the foot baths have been dry
for three years. The retail floor is a front and always was. The business is the ripper
suite in the basement, which has better lights than any hospital in the district and
worse wiring than the building it's under.

It smells like acetone over antiseptic. Everything is pink.

- **District**: Watson, Charter Street. Two blocks off the expressway on-ramp.
- **Who runs it**: **Mari Quist**, a ripperdoc struck off a real hospital, cuts here
  four nights a week. She does not own the building and does not know who does.
- **Who comes here**: gangers who can't go to a clinic, boosters getting cheap chrome
  seated, and anyone who needs a body looked at without a name attached to the looking.
- **House rules**: cash up front, no chrome bought on the premises, and Mari will not
  work on anyone she thinks is under sixteen. That last one is the only rule she has
  never broken.
- **What the crew can get here**: surgery at a third of the going rate with twice the
  infection risk, a cryo bay that actually works, and a back way into the storm drains
  that almost nobody knows about.

- **Four ways in**: the front door, the alley roll-up door, the storm drain into Room B,
  and the Net (the front lock and cameras are on a thin local node).
- **The generator** sits in the alley on a 40-amp run, cable fed through a broken
  basement window. It is the only thing keeping Room B cold.
- **The roof hatch** is painted shut. Anyone casing from above will assume it isn't.
