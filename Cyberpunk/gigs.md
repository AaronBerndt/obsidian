# Gigs

The running job board. Move a gig to **Completed** when it resolves, and note the
fallout — in Night City, every finished job leaves someone angry.

Format:
- **Gig** — one-line pitch
- **Client / Fixer** — who's paying, who brokered it
- **Pay** — eurodollars, favors, chrome, or data
- **Target** — the mark, the site, or the thing to be moved
- **Opposition** — who pushes back
- **Twist** — the thing the client didn't mention
